## Unreal past
### Regla y forma
- **EN:** **Wish/if only + past simple** expresses a present wish; **wish/if only + past perfect**, a past regret. **Would rather + subject + past simple** and **it's time + subject + past simple** express a preference or that it is already time to act.
- **ES:** **Wish/if only + past simple** expresa un deseo presente; **wish/if only + past perfect**, un lamento pasado. **Would rather + sujeto + past simple** y **it's time + sujeto + past simple** expresan una preferencia o que ya es hora de actuar.
### Formación
- **Name (EN):** Wish / if only (present wish) --- **Pattern:** Wish/if only + past simple.
- **Name (EN):** Wish / if only (past regret) --- **Pattern:** Wish/if only + past perfect.
- **Name (EN):** Would rather --- **Pattern:** Would rather + subject + past simple.
- **Name (EN):** It's time --- **Pattern:** It's time + subject + past simple.
### Definición
- **EN:** **Unreal past** means using a past verb form to express distance from reality, not necessarily past time. **Wish/if only + past simple** imagines a different present (I wish I knew); **wish/if only + past perfect** regrets an earlier event (I wish I had known). **Would rather + another subject + past simple** expresses a preference about what that person does (I'd rather you stayed), while **it's time + subject + past simple** says an action should happen now (It's time we left). These forms share a past shape, but their meanings and time references differ.
- **ES:** **Unreal past** significa usar una forma verbal pasada para marcar distancia de la realidad, no necesariamente tiempo pasado. **Wish/if only + past simple** imagina un presente distinto (I wish I knew); **wish/if only + past perfect** lamenta un hecho anterior (I wish I had known). **Would rather + otro sujeto + past simple** expresa una preferencia sobre lo que hace esa persona (I'd rather you stayed); **it's time + sujeto + past simple** dice que una acción ya debería ocurrir (It's time we left). Comparten forma pasada, pero difieren en significado y referencia temporal.
### Usos
- **EN:** Use wish to express a desire contrary to reality and if only to give it extra emphasis. It's time + subject + past simple indicates an action should already happen: It's time we left. In hypothetical wishes with be, were is common for every person, especially in more careful registers. Wish + would can ask someone to change a behaviour, but it is not normally used to change your own ability.
- **ES:** Usá wish para expresar un deseo contrario a la realidad e if only para darle más énfasis. It's time más sujeto más past simple indica que una acción ya debería ocurrir: It's time we left. En deseos hipotéticos con be, were es frecuente para todas las personas, especialmente en registros más cuidados. Wish más would puede expresar que alguien cambie un comportamiento, pero no se usa normalmente para cambiar una capacidad propia.
### Contrastes y errores
- **EN:** I wish I knew the answer says I do not know it now; I wish I had known the answer regrets not having known it before. After would rather with another subject, use the past simple: I'd rather you stayed, not I'd rather you stay in this pattern. It's time we went does not claim we already went: it says we should go now. Do not confuse the past form with the real time of the situation.
- **ES:** I wish I knew the answer expresa que ahora no la sé; I wish I had known the answer lamenta no haberla sabido antes. Después de would rather con otro sujeto se usa past simple: I'd rather you stayed, no I'd rather you stay en este patrón. It's time we went no afirma que ya fuimos: plantea que debemos ir ahora. No confundas la forma pasada con el tiempo real de la situación.
### Ejemplos
- **EN:** I wish I spoke another language fluently. --- **ES:** Ojalá hablara otro idioma con fluidez.
- **EN:** If only we had taken a map yesterday. --- **ES:** Ojalá hubiéramos llevado un mapa ayer.
- **EN:** It's time they started the meeting. --- **ES:** Ya es hora de que empiecen la reunión.
### Ejercicios
- **Prompt (EN):** You cannot drive, but you wish you had that ability now. Express the present wish with could. --- **Model (EN):** I wish I could drive. --- **Explanation (ES):** *Wish* más *could* expresa el deseo irreal de tener una capacidad en el presente. --- **Translation (ES):** Ojalá supiera manejar.
- **Prompt (EN):** Yesterday you forgot to call a friend and now regret it. Express your past regret with if only. --- **Model (EN):** If only I had called my friend yesterday. --- **Explanation (ES):** *If only* más *past perfect* expresa un lamento por algo que no ocurrió en el pasado. --- **Translation (ES):** Ojalá hubiera llamado a mi amigo ayer.
- **Prompt (EN):** You do not live near your family, but you would like that to be different now. Use wish. --- **Model (EN):** I wish I lived closer to my family. --- **Explanation (ES):** El *past simple* después de *wish* representa una situación presente contraria a la realidad. --- **Translation (ES):** Ojalá viviera más cerca de mi familia.
- **Prompt (EN):** It is already late, and your friends should go home now. Say so using it's time and a past form. --- **Model (EN):** It's time my friends went home. --- **Explanation (ES):** *It's time* más sujeto y *past simple* indica que la acción ya debería suceder. --- **Translation (ES):** Ya es hora de que mis amigos se vayan a casa.
- **Prompt (EN):** You prefer that your roommate does not play loud music tonight. Express your preference with would rather. --- **Model (EN):** I would rather my roommate didn't play loud music tonight. --- **Explanation (ES):** *Would rather* más otro sujeto lleva *past simple* para expresar una preferencia presente o futura. --- **Translation (ES):** Preferiría que mi compañero de casa no pusiera música fuerte esta noche.

## Conditional Sentences: Type 3
### Regla y forma
- **EN:** The **third conditional** combines **if + past perfect** in the condition with **would/could/might have + past participle** in the result. Both parts describe an unreal alternative to the past.
- **ES:** El **third conditional** combina **if + past perfect** en la condición con **would/could/might have + participio pasado** en el resultado. Ambas partes describen una alternativa irreal al pasado.
### Formación
- **Name (EN):** Third conditional
  - **Condition:** if + subject + had + past participle
  - **Result:** subject + would/could/might have + past participle
### Definición
- **EN:** The **third conditional** imagines an alternative to an event that is already over. The if clause names a past condition that did not occur (If I had left earlier); the main clause imagines its unreal past consequence (I would have caught the train). **Could have** highlights an unreal possibility or ability; **might have** makes the imagined result less certain. Unlike type 2, which imagines a different present or future, type 3 cannot change what actually happened in the past.
- **ES:** El **third conditional** imagina una alternativa a un hecho que ya terminó. La cláusula con if plantea una condición pasada que no se cumplió (If I had left earlier); la principal imagina su consecuencia pasada irreal (I would have caught the train). **Could have** destaca una posibilidad o capacidad no concretada; **might have** presenta el resultado imaginado como menos seguro. A diferencia del tipo 2, que imagina un presente o futuro distinto, el tipo 3 no puede cambiar lo que efectivamente pasó.
### Usos
- **EN:** Use type 3 to imagine what would have happened if a past condition had been different, to express regret or to explain a missed opportunity. Could have + participle expresses past ability or possibility in the result; might have marks a less certain consequence. If both people know the condition, had and would can be contracted in speech and informal writing.
- **ES:** Usá el tipo 3 para imaginar qué habría ocurrido si una condición pasada hubiera sido distinta, expresar un arrepentimiento o explicar una oportunidad perdida. Could have más participio expresa capacidad o posibilidad pasada en el resultado; might have señala una consecuencia menos segura. Si ambas personas conocen la condición, se pueden abreviar had y would en el habla y la escritura informal.
### Contrastes y errores
- **EN:** If I had left earlier, I would have arrived on time does not mean I left early: both situations are hypothetical. Do not use would have in the if clause of the standard pattern: if I had known, not if I would have known. Do not confuse type 3 with type 2: type 3 imagines changes to past facts and uses past perfect + would have.
- **ES:** If I had left earlier, I would have arrived on time no significa que salí temprano: ambas situaciones son hipotéticas. No uses would have en la cláusula con if del patrón estándar: if I had known, no if I would have known. No confundas el tipo 3 con el tipo 2: el tipo 3 imagina cambios en hechos pasados y usa past perfect más would have.
### Ejemplos
- **EN:** If she had checked the address, she would have found the house. --- **ES:** Si hubiera revisado la dirección, habría encontrado la casa.
- **EN:** We could have joined them if we had finished earlier. --- **ES:** Podríamos habernos sumado si hubiéramos terminado antes.
- **EN:** If the bus had come on time, they might have caught the train. --- **ES:** Si el colectivo hubiera llegado a tiempo, quizás habrían alcanzado el tren.
### Ejercicios
- **Prompt (EN):** You missed the train because you woke up late. Imagine the opposite past using if and would have. --- **Model (EN):** If I had woken up earlier, I would have caught the train. --- **Explanation (ES):** El tipo 3 combina *past perfect* para la condición irreal con *would have* más participio para el resultado pasado. --- **Translation (ES):** Si me hubiera despertado más temprano, habría alcanzado el tren.
- **Prompt (EN):** Your friend did not study, so they failed. Say what would have happened if they had studied. --- **Model (EN):** If my friend had studied, they would have passed. --- **Explanation (ES):** La condición que no ocurrió va con *had studied*; su resultado hipotético, con *would have passed*. --- **Translation (ES):** Si mi amigo hubiera estudiado, habría aprobado.
- **Prompt (EN):** You did not have a ticket, so you could not attend the concert. Express the lost opportunity with could have. --- **Model (EN):** If I had had a ticket, I could have attended the concert. --- **Explanation (ES):** *Had had* es el *past perfect* de *have*; *could have attended* destaca la posibilidad pasada perdida. --- **Translation (ES):** Si hubiera tenido una entrada, podría haber ido al concierto.
- **Prompt (EN):** It rained during the picnic, so you stayed inside. Suggest a less certain alternative with might have. --- **Model (EN):** If it hadn't rained, we might have had the picnic outside. --- **Explanation (ES):** *Might have* expresa que el resultado pasado era posible, pero no seguro. --- **Translation (ES):** Si no hubiera llovido, quizás habríamos hecho el picnic afuera.
- **Prompt (EN):** Your sister did not tell you about the meeting, so you did not go. Start with the hypothetical result. --- **Model (EN):** I would have gone to the meeting if my sister had told me about it. --- **Explanation (ES):** La consecuencia puede ir antes de la condición; en ese orden no se necesita coma. --- **Translation (ES):** Habría ido a la reunión si mi hermana me hubiera avisado.

## Causative Form
### Regla y forma
- **EN:** **Have/get + object + past participle** says a job was arranged. To name who did it, use **have + person + base verb** or **get + person + to + base verb**.
- **ES:** **Have/get + objeto + participio pasado** indica que se encargó un trabajo. Para nombrar quién lo hizo, usá **have + persona + verbo base** o **get + persona + to + verbo base**.
### Formación
- **Name (EN):** Have/get something done --- **Pattern:** Subject + have/get + object + past participle.
- **Name (EN):** Have someone do something --- **Pattern:** Subject + have + person + base verb.
- **Name (EN):** Get someone to do something --- **Pattern:** Subject + get + person + to + base verb.
### Definición
- **EN:** The **causative form** distinguishes doing a job yourself from arranging for someone else to do it: I repaired my car says I did the work; I had my car repaired says someone else did it. **Have/get + thing + past participle** focuses on the service or result; **have + person + base verb** or **get + person + to + base verb** names the person performing the action. The first pattern can also describe an unwanted experience (She had her phone stolen), so the context determines whether the event was arranged.
- **ES:** La **causative form** distingue entre hacer un trabajo personalmente y conseguir que otra persona lo haga: I repaired my car dice que yo lo hice; I had my car repaired dice que lo hizo alguien más. **Have/get + cosa + participio** pone el foco en el servicio o resultado; **have + persona + verbo base** o **get + persona + to + verbo base** nombra a quien realiza la acción. El primer patrón también puede describir una experiencia no deseada (She had her phone stolen), así que el contexto determina si el hecho fue encargado.
### Usos
- **EN:** Use the causative form when you arrange for another person to do a job for you or on something of yours, without claiming you did it yourself. Have can be conjugated in the past, present or future: had the roof fixed, have the windows cleaned, will have the room painted. Get sometimes stresses the effort of making the job happen. The context can also describe something that happens to you against your will, like having your wallet stolen.
- **ES:** Usá la causative form cuando organizás que otra persona haga un trabajo para vos o sobre algo tuyo, sin afirmar que lo hiciste personalmente. Have puede conjugarse en pasado, presente o futuro: had the roof fixed, have the windows cleaned, will have the room painted. Get enfatiza a veces el esfuerzo de conseguir que el trabajo se realice. El contexto también puede describir algo que te ocurre sin que lo hayas querido, como having your wallet stolen.
### Contrastes y errores
- **EN:** I repaired my bike says I fixed it; I had my bike repaired indicates another person did it. In have something done use the participle: had the door painted, not had the door paint. If the object is a person performing the action, the pattern changes: had the mechanic check the brakes; with get, got the mechanic to check the brakes.
- **ES:** I repaired my bike dice que yo lo arreglé; I had my bike repaired indica que otra persona lo hizo. En have something done se usa el participio: had the door painted, no had the door paint. Si el objeto es una persona que realiza la acción, el patrón cambia: had the mechanic check the brakes; con get, got the mechanic to check the brakes.
### Ejemplos
- **EN:** She had her kitchen painted last month. --- **ES:** Hizo pintar su cocina el mes pasado.
- **EN:** We got our internet connection fixed yesterday. --- **ES:** Hicimos arreglar nuestra conexión a internet ayer.
- **EN:** He had the technician inspect the machine. --- **ES:** Le pidió al técnico que revisara la máquina.
### Ejercicios
- **Prompt (EN):** A mechanic repaired your car yesterday; you did not do it yourself. Describe the arranged service with had. --- **Model (EN):** I had my car repaired yesterday. --- **Explanation (ES):** *Had* más objeto más participio indica que otra persona realizó el arreglo. --- **Translation (ES):** Hice arreglar mi auto ayer.
- **Prompt (EN):** A photographer took your portrait last week. Describe the service with got and a past participle. --- **Model (EN):** I got my portrait taken last week. --- **Explanation (ES):** *Got* más objeto más participio presenta un servicio hecho por otra persona. --- **Translation (ES):** Me hice sacar un retrato la semana pasada.
- **Prompt (EN):** You will arrange for someone to clean your windows tomorrow. Express the future plan with will have. --- **Model (EN):** I will have my windows cleaned tomorrow. --- **Explanation (ES):** *Will have* más objeto más participio sitúa el servicio encargado en el futuro. --- **Translation (ES):** Mañana voy a hacer limpiar mis ventanas.
- **Prompt (EN):** You asked the plumber to check the pipes, and the plumber did it. Name the person with had someone do. --- **Model (EN):** I had the plumber check the pipes. --- **Explanation (ES):** *Have* más persona más verbo base indica quién realizó el trabajo encargado. --- **Translation (ES):** Hice que el plomero revisara las cañerías.
- **Prompt (EN):** You persuaded your brother to help you move the sofa. Name him using got someone to do. --- **Model (EN):** I got my brother to help me move the sofa. --- **Explanation (ES):** *Get* más persona más infinitivo con *to* destaca que conseguiste su colaboración. --- **Translation (ES):** Logré que mi hermano me ayudara a mover el sofá.
