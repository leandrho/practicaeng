## Passive Voice
### Regla y forma
- **EN:** The passive voice combines **a conjugated be + the past participle**; the tense is marked on be. The agent can be added with **by** when it matters to name it.
- **ES:** La voz pasiva combina **be conjugado + participio pasado**; el tiempo se marca en be. El agente puede agregarse con **by** cuando importa nombrarlo.
### Formación
- **Name (EN):** Passive Voice
  - **Affirmative:** subject receiving the action + be conjugated in the corresponding tense + past participle (+ by + agent)
  - **Negative:** subject receiving the action + be conjugated in the corresponding tense + not + past participle (+ by + agent)
  - **Question:** be conjugated in the corresponding tense + subject receiving the action + past participle (+ by + agent)?
### Usos
- **EN:** Use the passive when the result or whoever receives the action matters more than the agent, for example in news, instructions or process descriptions. Tense is marked by be: use is or are for the present simple, was or were for the past simple and will be for the future with will. In the present perfect, use have or has been plus the participle.
- **ES:** Usá la pasiva cuando el resultado o quien recibe la acción es más importante que el agente, por ejemplo en noticias, instrucciones o descripciones de procesos. El tiempo lo marca be: se usa is o are para present simple, was o were para past simple y will be para futuro con will. En present perfect se usa have o has been más participio.
### Contrastes y errores
- **EN:** The bridge was built in 1990 focuses on the bridge; The workers built the bridge focuses on the workers. Do not omit be: The bridge built in 1990 is not a complete passive sentence. After be goes the participle, not the base form: was written, not was write. In the present perfect passive do not omit been: has been delivered.
- **ES:** The bridge was built in 1990 pone el foco en el puente; The workers built the bridge pone el foco en los trabajadores. No omitas be: The bridge built in 1990 no es una oración pasiva completa. Después de be va el participio, no la forma base: was written, no was write. En la pasiva con present perfect no omitas been: has been delivered.
### Ejemplos
- **EN:** These bicycles are made in Portugal. --- **ES:** Estas bicicletas se fabrican en Portugal.
- **EN:** The window was broken during the storm. --- **ES:** La ventana se rompió durante la tormenta.
- **EN:** The invitations have been sent already. --- **ES:** Las invitaciones ya fueron enviadas.
### Ejercicios
- **Prompt (EN):** The bakery makes fresh bread every morning. Focus on the bread and use present simple passive. --- **Model (EN):** Fresh bread is made every morning. --- **Explanation (ES):** El objeto *bread* pasa a sujeto; *is made* forma la pasiva en *present simple* sin agente innecesario. --- **Translation (ES):** Todas las mañanas se hace pan fresco.
- **Prompt (EN):** Workers repaired the road last night. Focus on the road and use past simple passive. --- **Model (EN):** The road was repaired last night. --- **Explanation (ES):** *Was* más participio forma la pasiva en pasado; no hace falta mencionar a los trabajadores. --- **Translation (ES):** La ruta fue reparada anoche.
- **Prompt (EN):** The museum will display a new painting next month. Focus on the painting using future passive. --- **Model (EN):** A new painting will be displayed next month. --- **Explanation (ES):** *Will be* más participio expresa la pasiva de un hecho futuro. --- **Translation (ES):** El mes que viene se exhibirá una pintura nueva.
- **Prompt (EN):** Someone has delivered your package. Tell your friend that it has arrived using present perfect passive. --- **Model (EN):** My package has been delivered. --- **Explanation (ES):** *Has been* más participio forma la pasiva en *present perfect* y destaca el resultado actual. --- **Translation (ES):** Entregaron mi paquete.
- **Prompt (EN):** A famous architect designed the library in 2005. Make the library the subject but name the architect as the agent. --- **Model (EN):** The library was designed by a famous architect in 2005. --- **Explanation (ES):** La pasiva usa *was designed* y *by* introduce al agente cuando su identidad importa. --- **Translation (ES):** La biblioteca fue diseñada por un arquitecto famoso en 2005.

## Clauses of concession
### Regla y forma
- **EN:** **Although/though/even though + subject + verb** introduce a clause. **Despite/in spite of + noun or -ing form** presents the obstacle without a finite clause.
- **ES:** **Although/though/even though + sujeto + verbo** introduce una cláusula. **Despite/in spite of + sustantivo o verbo en -ing** presenta el obstáculo sin una cláusula finita.
### Formación
- **Name (EN):** Although / though / even though --- **Pattern:** Although/though/even though + subject + verb.
- **Name (EN):** Despite / in spite of --- **Pattern:** Despite/in spite of + noun or -ing form.
### Usos
- **EN:** Use although for a general contrast and even though to stress that the result is surprising despite a real difficulty. Despite or in spite of let you condense the obstacle into a noun phrase: despite the noise; in spite of feeling tired. Despite the fact that and in spite of the fact that do allow a subject and verb after that.
- **ES:** Usá although para un contraste general y even though para subrayar que el resultado sorprende pese a una dificultad real. Despite o in spite of permiten condensar el obstáculo en una frase nominal: despite the noise; in spite of feeling tired. Despite the fact that e in spite of the fact that sí admiten sujeto y verbo después de that.
### Contrastes y errores
- **EN:** Although the train was late is correct, but despite the train was late is not: say despite the delay or despite the fact that the train was late. Do not combine although with but to join the same two clauses: Although it rained, we played; or It rained, but we played. If the subordinate clause comes first, separate it from the main clause with a comma.
- **ES:** Although the train was late es correcto, pero despite the train was late no: decí despite the delay o despite the fact that the train was late. No combines although con but para unir las mismas dos cláusulas: Although it rained, we played; o It rained, but we played. Si la subordinada va primero, separala de la principal con una coma.
### Ejemplos
- **EN:** Although the café was crowded, we found a table. --- **ES:** Aunque el café estaba lleno, encontramos una mesa.
- **EN:** Despite the cold weather, they went swimming. --- **ES:** A pesar del frío, fueron a nadar.
- **EN:** Even though she was tired, she finished the presentation. --- **ES:** Aunque estaba cansada, terminó la presentación.
### Ejercicios
- **Prompt (EN):** It was raining, but you and a friend went for a walk. Start the sentence with although. --- **Model (EN):** Although it was raining, we went for a walk. --- **Explanation (ES):** *Although* introduce una cláusula con sujeto y verbo; la principal expresa el resultado inesperado. --- **Translation (ES):** Aunque estaba lloviendo, salimos a caminar.
- **Prompt (EN):** The noise was loud, but your brother slept. Start with despite and use a noun phrase. --- **Model (EN):** Despite the loud noise, my brother slept. --- **Explanation (ES):** *Despite* va seguido de un sintagma nominal, no de una cláusula finita. --- **Translation (ES):** A pesar del ruido fuerte, mi hermano durmió.
- **Prompt (EN):** You felt nervous, but you gave the speech. Use in spite of followed by an -ing form. --- **Model (EN):** In spite of feeling nervous, I gave the speech. --- **Explanation (ES):** *In spite of* puede preceder a una forma *-ing* para expresar el obstáculo. --- **Translation (ES):** A pesar de estar nervioso, di el discurso.
- **Prompt (EN):** The exam was difficult, but your friend passed. Emphasize the unexpected success with even though. --- **Model (EN):** Even though the exam was difficult, my friend passed. --- **Explanation (ES):** *Even though* introduce una cláusula y enfatiza la concesión frente a un resultado sorprendente. --- **Translation (ES):** Aunque el examen fue difícil, mi amigo aprobó.
- **Prompt (EN):** The bus was delayed, but the team arrived on time. Use despite the fact that followed by a full clause. --- **Model (EN):** Despite the fact that the bus was delayed, the team arrived on time. --- **Explanation (ES):** *Despite the fact that* admite una cláusula completa con sujeto y verbo. --- **Translation (ES):** A pesar de que el colectivo se demoró, el equipo llegó a tiempo.
