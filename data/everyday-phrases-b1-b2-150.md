## Greetings & socializing

- **How's it going?** --- ask how someone is doing --- ¿Cómo va todo? --- *How's it going at your new job?* --- *¿Cómo va todo en tu nuevo trabajo?* --- *How's it going with the new house? We moved last week. Come visit on Sunday.* --- *Everyday conversation* --- *Tags: Informal, Spoken*
- **Nice to meet you** --- polite phrase when meeting someone for the first time --- Mucho gusto --- *Hi, I'm Paula. Nice to meet you.* --- *Hola, soy Paula. Mucho gusto.* --- *Hi, I'm Diego. Nice to meet you. Are you also new here?* --- *Everyday conversation* --- *Tags: Polite*
- **Long time no see** --- say that you have not seen someone for a long time --- Tanto tiempo --- *Long time no see! How have you been?* --- *¡Tanto tiempo! ¿Cómo estuviste?* --- *Long time no see! You look great. Let's catch up over coffee.* --- *Everyday conversation* --- *Tags: Informal, Spoken*
- **What have you been up to?** --- ask what someone has been doing recently --- ¿Qué estuviste haciendo? --- *What have you been up to since graduation?* --- *¿Qué estuviste haciendo desde que te recibiste?* --- *What have you been up to lately? I heard about your trip. Tell me everything.* --- *Everyday conversation* --- *Tags: Informal, Spoken*
- **How have you been?** --- ask about someone's recent situation --- ¿Cómo estuviste? --- *How have you been since you moved?* --- *¿Cómo estuviste desde que te mudaste?* --- *How have you been since January? We miss you at the club. Come back soon.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **It's good to see you** --- express happiness about seeing someone --- Qué bueno verte --- *It's good to see you again.* --- *Qué bueno verte de nuevo.* --- *It's good to see you here. The party is better now. Come meet my friends.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **Say hello to** --- send greetings to another person --- Decile hola a --- *Say hello to your sister for me.* --- *Decile hola a tu hermana de mi parte.* --- *Say hello to your brother for me. I hope his leg is better. See you Sunday.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **Give my regards to** --- ask someone to pass on respectful greetings --- Mandale saludos a --- *Give my regards to your parents.* --- *Mandale saludos a tus papás.* --- *Give my regards to your mom. Her cake was amazing. I want the recipe.* --- *Everyday conversation* --- *Tags: Formal, Polite*
- **Make yourself at home** --- tell a guest to feel comfortable --- Sentite como en casa --- *Make yourself at home while I make some tea.* --- *Sentite como en casa mientras preparo un té.* --- *I am bashful, though no one will believe it. “Now, my dear, make yourself at home,” said Mrs. K. in her motherly way, “I’m on the drive from morning to night, as you may suppose with such a family, but a great anxiety will be off my mind if I know the children are safe with you.* --- *Little Women — Louisa May Alcott* --- *Tags: Polite*
- **Help yourself** --- invite someone to take what they want --- Servite --- *There's cake in the kitchen, help yourself.* --- *Hay torta en la cocina, servite.* --- *“Hawkins hasn’t had his breakfast. Hawkins, help yourself, and back to your post to eat it,” continued Captain Smollett.* --- *Treasure Island — Robert Louis Stevenson* --- *Tags: Informal, Polite*
- **Mind if I join you?** --- ask permission to sit or take part with someone --- ¿Te molesta si me sumo? --- *Mind if I join you for lunch?* --- *¿Te molesta si me sumo al almuerzo?* --- *Mind if I join you guys? I finished my work early. I brought snacks.* --- *Everyday conversation* --- *Tags: Polite*
- **I was just leaving** --- say that you were about to go --- Justo me estaba yendo --- *I was just leaving when you called.* --- *Justo me estaba yendo cuando llamaste.* --- *I was just leaving for the store. Do you need anything? I can buy extra milk.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **See you around** --- casual way to say goodbye when you expect to meet again --- Nos vemos por ahí --- *I have to go now. See you around.* --- *Me tengo que ir. Nos vemos por ahí.* --- *I must run to class. See you around the gym. We train Thursday, right?* --- *Everyday conversation* --- *Tags: Informal, Spoken*
- **Take care** --- friendly way to say goodbye and wish someone well --- Cuidate --- *Take care and text me when you get home.* --- *Cuidate y escribime cuando llegues a casa.* --- *Take care on your trip. Call when you land. We will miss you.* --- *Everyday conversation* --- *Tags: Informal, Spoken*
- **Have a good one** --- casual way to wish someone a good day --- Que tengas un buen día --- *Thanks for your help. Have a good one!* --- *Gracias por tu ayuda. ¡Que tengas un buen día!* --- *Bye, have a good one! Enjoy the concert. Tell me about it later.* --- *Everyday conversation* --- *Tags: Informal, Spoken*
- **Sleep well** --- wish someone a restful night --- Que duermas bien --- *Good night, sleep well.* --- *Buenas noches, que duermas bien.* --- *Sleep well and rest. Tomorrow is a big day. Good night, Ana.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **Enjoy your weekend** --- wish someone a pleasant weekend --- Que disfrutes el fin de semana --- *See you Monday. Enjoy your weekend.* --- *Nos vemos el lunes. Que disfrutes el fin de semana.* --- *Enjoy your weekend at the lake. The weather looks fine. Send photos, please.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **Good luck with** --- wish someone success in an activity --- Suerte con --- *Good luck with your exam tomorrow.* --- *Suerte con tu examen de mañana.* --- *Good luck with the interview! You prepared a lot. They will love you.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **Happy birthday** --- congratulate someone on the day they were born --- Feliz cumpleaños --- *Happy birthday! I hope you have a great day.* --- *¡Feliz cumpleaños! Espero que tengas un día genial.* --- *Happy birthday, Lucy! I made your cake. Blow the candles now.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **Congratulations on** --- praise someone for an achievement or event --- Felicitaciones por --- *Congratulations on your new apartment.* --- *Felicitaciones por tu departamento nuevo.* --- *Congratulations on the baby! She is beautiful. When can we visit?* --- *Everyday conversation* --- *Tags: Polite*
- **Sorry I'm late** --- apologize for arriving after the expected time --- Perdón por llegar tarde --- *Sorry I'm late, the train was delayed.* --- *Perdón por llegar tarde, el tren se demoró.* --- *Sorry I'm late, the bus broke. I ran from the corner. Did I miss much?* --- *Everyday conversation* --- *Tags: Polite*
- **No worries** --- say that something is not a problem --- No hay problema --- *Sorry I forgot. No worries, it happens.* --- *Perdón, me olvidé. No hay problema, pasa.* --- *Dear me! How happy and good we’d be, if we had no worries!” said Meg, who could remember better times.* --- *Little Women — Louisa May Alcott* --- *Tags: Informal, Spoken*
- **That's a shame** --- express sympathy about an unfortunate situation --- Qué lástima --- *You can't come tonight? That's a shame.* --- *¿No podés venir esta noche? Qué lástima.* --- *He lost his wallet? That's a shame. It had his cards. Let's help him look.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **What a surprise** --- express surprise at an unexpected event --- Qué sorpresa --- *You're here already? What a surprise!* --- *¿Ya estás acá? ¡Qué sorpresa!* --- *What a surprise to see you! I thought you traveled. Stay for dinner.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **I can't believe it** --- express strong surprise or disbelief --- No lo puedo creer --- *I can't believe it, we won!* --- *No lo puedo creer, ¡ganamos!* --- *I can't believe it, you won! All that practice helped. We celebrate tonight.* --- *Everyday conversation* --- *Tags: Informal, Spoken*

## Courtesy & requests

It is too heavy alone. It goes to the back room.* --- *Everyday conversation* --- *Tags: Polite*
- **Would you mind waiting?** --- politely ask someone to wait --- ¿Te molestaría esperar? --- *Would you mind waiting for five minutes?* --- *¿Te molestaría esperar cinco minutos?* --- *Would you mind waiting outside? The doctor is almost free. It takes five minutes.* --- *Everyday conversation* --- *Tags: Formal, Polite*
- **Can I ask you a favor?** --- ask permission to request help --- ¿Te puedo pedir un favor? --- *Can I ask you a favor before you leave?* --- *¿Te puedo pedir un favor antes de que te vayas?* --- *Can I ask you a favor for Friday? I need a ride early. My car is broken.* --- *Everyday conversation* --- *Tags: Polite*
- **Do you mind if I sit here?** --- ask permission to use a seat --- ¿Te molesta si me siento acá? --- *Do you mind if I sit here by the window?* --- *¿Te molesta si me siento acá, junto a la ventana?* --- *Do you mind if I sit here? The other tables are full. I will leave soon.* --- *Everyday conversation* --- *Tags: Polite*
- **I'd appreciate it if you could** --- politely introduce a request --- Te agradecería si pudieras --- *I'd appreciate it if you could send the address.* --- *Te agradecería si pudieras mandar la dirección.* --- *I'd appreciate it if you could call me. My phone has no sound. I miss every call.* --- *Everyday conversation* --- *Tags: Formal, Polite*
- **After you** --- invite another person to go first --- Después de vos --- *After you, please.* --- *Después de vos, por favor.* --- *After you, please. The door is heavy. I follow with the bags.* --- *Everyday conversation* --- *Tags: Polite*
- **Excuse me** --- politely get someone's attention or apologize --- Disculpá --- *Excuse me, is this seat free?* --- *Disculpá, ¿este asiento está libre?* --- *You’ll excuse me, I dare say, but you remind me of that fable. - **Pardon me** --- politely apologize or ask someone to repeat something --- Perdón --- *Pardon me, could you repeat that?* --- *Perdón, ¿podrías repetir eso?* --- *Pardon me, I didn't hear you. The music is loud. Can you speak up?* --- *Everyday conversation* --- *Tags: Formal, Polite*
- **I'm sorry to bother you** --- apologize before interrupting someone --- Perdón que te moleste --- *I'm sorry to bother you, but do you have a minute?* --- *Perdón que te moleste, pero ¿tenés un minuto?* --- *I'm sorry to bother you so late. My keys are inside. Can you open the door?* --- *Everyday conversation* --- *Tags: Formal, Polite*
- **Could I have a receipt?** --- politely request proof of payment --- ¿Me podrías dar el ticket? --- *Could I have a receipt, please?* --- *¿Me podrías dar el ticket, por favor?* --- *Could I have a receipt for this? I need it for work. They pay my travel.* --- *Everyday conversation* --- *Tags: Polite*
- **May I come in?** --- politely ask permission to enter --- ¿Puedo pasar? --- *May I come in, or are you busy?* --- *¿Puedo pasar o estás ocupado?* --- *“May I come in, please? Or shall I be a bother?” he asked, advancing slowly.* --- *Little Women — Louisa May Alcott* --- *Tags: Formal, Polite*
- **Would it be possible to reschedule?** --- ask to change an arranged time --- ¿Sería posible reprogramar? --- *Would it be possible to reschedule our appointment?* --- *¿Sería posible reprogramar nuestro turno?* --- *Would it be possible to reschedule Tuesday? My son is sick. Thursday works for me.* --- *Everyday conversation* --- *Tags: Formal, Polite*
- **Thanks anyway** --- thank someone even though they could not help --- Gracias igual --- *You don't have any left? Thanks anyway.* --- *¿No te queda ninguno? Gracias igual.* --- *No more tickets? Thanks anyway. We will try online. See you tomorrow.* --- *Everyday conversation* --- *Tags: Polite*
- **You're welcome** --- polite reply to thanks --- De nada --- *Thanks for explaining it. You're welcome.* --- *Gracias por explicarlo. De nada.* --- *Thanks for the ride! You're welcome anytime. It was on my way.* --- *Everyday conversation* --- *Tags: Polite*
- **Don't mention it** --- modest reply to thanks --- No es nada --- *Thanks for staying late. Don't mention it.* --- *Gracias por quedarte hasta tarde. No es nada.* --- *Thanks for the cookies! Don't mention it, I love baking. Take more for home.* --- *Everyday conversation* --- *Tags: Informal, Polite*
- **My pleasure** --- warm reply to thanks --- Es un placer --- *Thank you for your help. My pleasure.* --- *Gracias por tu ayuda. Es un placer.* --- *Thanks for coming early. My pleasure, I like mornings. Coffee is ready.* --- *Everyday conversation* --- *Tags: Formal, Polite*
- **That's very kind of you** --- express gratitude for a considerate action --- Es muy amable de tu parte --- *You brought me coffee? That's very kind of you.* --- *¿Me trajiste café? Es muy amable de tu parte.* --- *You fixed my bike? That's very kind of you. How can I thank you?* --- *Everyday conversation* --- *Tags: Polite*
- **I owe you one** --- say you will return a favor later --- Te debo una --- *Thanks for the ride. I owe you one.* --- *Gracias por llevarme. Te debo una.* --- *Thanks for the airport ride. I owe you one. Dinner is on me Friday.* --- *Everyday conversation* --- *Tags: Informal, Spoken*
- **Could you do me a favor?** --- politely ask someone to help with something --- ¿Me harías un favor? --- *Could you do me a favor and close the door?* --- *¿Me harías un favor y cerrás la puerta?* --- *Could you do me a favor and feed the dog? - **Let me know** --- ask someone to give you information later --- Avisame --- *Let me know when you arrive.* --- *Avisame cuando llegues.* --- *Windibank sprang out of his chair and picked up his hat. “I cannot waste time over this sort of fantastic talk, Mr. Holmes,” he said. “If you can catch the man, catch him, and let me know when you have done it.”* --- *The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes — Arthur Conan Doyle*
- **Feel free to ask** --- invite someone to ask questions without concern --- No dudes en preguntar --- *Feel free to ask if anything is unclear.* --- *No dudes en preguntar si algo no está claro.* --- *Feel free to ask anything. No question is silly. I am here to help.* --- *Everyday conversation* --- *Tags: Polite*
- **There's no rush** --- tell someone there is no need to hurry --- No hay apuro --- *There's no rush, I'm not in a hurry.* --- *No hay apuro, no estoy apurado.* --- *There's no rush with the payment. Next week is fine. Enjoy your holidays first.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **Whenever you're ready** --- say that someone can proceed at any time --- Cuando estés listo --- *Whenever you're ready, we can start.* --- *Cuando estés listo, podemos empezar.* --- *Whenever you're ready, tell me. The car waits outside. No hurry at all.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **That would be great** --- say that a suggestion would be very helpful or welcome --- Eso estaría buenísimo --- *Can I send it tonight? That would be great.* --- *¿Te lo puedo mandar esta noche? Eso estaría buenísimo.* --- *Can you bring dessert? That would be great. We have drinks already.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **I really appreciate it** --- express strong gratitude --- Te lo agradezco mucho --- *I really appreciate it, thanks.* --- *Te lo agradezco mucho, gracias.* --- *I really appreciate it, Ana. You saved my day. I won't forget this.* --- *Everyday conversation* --- *Tags: Polite*
