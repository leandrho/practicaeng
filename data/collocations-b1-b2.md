# Collocations esenciales B1--B2

Colección para tarjetas de práctica. Intentá completar o explicar la expresión antes de revelar la respuesta.

## MAKE
- **make a decision** --- choose what to do --- tomar una decisión --- *I need to make a decision today.* --- *Necesito tomar una decisión hoy.* --- *You need to make a decision today. The offer ends tonight. Call me after you think.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **make a mistake** --- do something incorrectly --- cometer un error --- *Everyone makes mistakes.* --- *Todos cometemos errores.* --- *Everyone makes mistakes at first. I broke a glass yesterday. Now I am more careful.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **make an effort** --- try hard --- hacer un esfuerzo --- *You need to make an effort.* --- *Tenés que hacer un esfuerzo.* --- *You ought to make an effort and go visiting everywhere you are asked, then you’ll have plenty of friends, and pleasant places to go to. Never mind being bashful.* --- *Little Women — Louisa May Alcott*
- **make progress** --- improve or advance --- progresar --- *You're making good progress.* --- *Estás progresando mucho.* --- *My son is making progress in maths. He studies half an hour daily. His teacher is pleased.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **make money** --- earn money --- ganar dinero --- *She makes money online.* --- *Ella gana dinero en línea.* --- *She makes money selling cakes. Her shop opens early. Neighbors buy everything.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **make a difference** --- have an important effect --- marcar una diferencia --- *Small changes can make a difference.* --- *Los cambios pequeños pueden marcar una diferencia.* --- *One teacher can make a difference. Mr. Ruiz helped me believe. Now I study with joy.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **make a choice** --- choose between options --- hacer una elección --- *You have to make a choice.* --- *Tenés que hacer una elección.* --- *There are three films tonight. Help me make a choice. I trust your taste.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **make a promise** --- say that you will do something --- hacer una promesa --- *I made a promise to my son.* --- *Le hice una promesa a mi hijo.* --- *Dad made a promise to us. We travel south in winter. He already bought the tickets.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **make friends** --- become friends with people --- hacer amigos --- *It's easy to make friends there.* --- *Es fácil hacer amigos ahí.* --- *She made friends quickly in the new street. The girls play after school. Now they are best friends.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **make plans** --- arrange future activities --- hacer planes --- *We're making plans for the weekend.* --- *Estamos haciendo planes para el fin de semana.* --- *Don't make plans for Saturday. We visit Grandma together. She cooks our favorite food.* --- *Everyday conversation*

## DO
- **do homework** --- complete schoolwork --- hacer la tarea --- *I have to do my homework.* --- *Tengo que hacer la tarea.* --- *The kids do homework at five. Then they watch cartoons. Bed is at nine sharp.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **do exercise** --- take physical exercise --- hacer ejercicio --- *I do exercise three times a week.* --- *Hago ejercicio tres veces por semana.* --- *We do exercise in the park. There is a free class. It starts at eight.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **do business** --- conduct commercial activity --- hacer negocios --- *We do business with several companies.* --- *Hacemos negocios con varias empresas.* --- *Windibank, asking him whether he could meet us here at six o’clock to-morrow evening. It is just as well that we should do business with the male relatives.* --- *The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes — Arthur Conan Doyle*
- **do your best** --- try as hard as possible --- hacer lo mejor posible --- *Just do your best.* --- *Simplemente hacé lo mejor que puedas.* --- *You should do just what your grandfather wishes, my dear boy,” said Meg in her most maternal tone. “Do your best at college, and when he sees that you try to please him, I’m sure he won’t be hard on you or unjust to you.* --- *Little Women — Louisa May Alcott*
- **do someone a favor** --- do something helpful for someone --- hacerle un favor a alguien --- *Can you do me a favor?* --- *¿Me podés hacer un favor?* --- *Could you do me a favor and water the plants? I travel Friday. The keys are with Ana.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **do research** --- study a subject to discover information --- investigar --- *They're doing research on the topic.* --- *Están investigando el tema.* --- *I do research for my videos. I read three books weekly. My channel grows fast.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **do the shopping** --- buy food and household items --- hacer las compras --- *I'll do the shopping tonight.* --- *Voy a hacer las compras esta noche.* --- *Mom does the shopping on Mondays. She goes early by bus. The market is cheap then.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **do the dishes** --- wash dishes --- lavar los platos --- *I'll do the dishes.* --- *Voy a lavar los platos.* --- *You cook and I'll do the dishes. It takes ten minutes. Then we watch the film.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **do a good job** --- perform a task well --- hacer un buen trabajo --- *You did a good job.* --- *Hiciste un buen trabajo.* --- *You did a good job with the garden. The flowers look lovely. Thanks for your help.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **do damage** --- cause harm --- causar daño --- *The storm did a lot of damage.* --- *La tormenta causó mucho daño.* --- *The rain did damage to the roof. Water came inside. We need a repair fast.* --- *Everyday conversation*

## HAVE / TAKE / GET
- **have a good time** --- enjoy yourself --- pasarla bien --- *We had a good time.* --- *La pasamos bien.* --- *Did you have a good time last night? The film was funny. We should go again next week.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **have a conversation** --- talk with someone --- tener una conversación --- *We had a long conversation.* --- *Tuvimos una conversación larga.* --- *We had a long conversation over coffee. Two hours passed fast. We solved everything.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **have a chance** --- have an opportunity --- tener una oportunidad --- *I finally have a chance to practice.* --- *Por fin tengo la oportunidad de practicar.* --- *She has a chance to study abroad. The school pays everything. Her parents are happy.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **take a break** --- stop working to rest --- tomarse un descanso --- *Let's take a break.* --- *Tomemos un descanso.* --- *We worked all morning. Let's take a break outside. The sun feels good.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **take a look** --- look briefly --- echar un vistazo --- *Take a look at this.* --- *Echá un vistazo a esto.* --- *Come and take a look outside. There is a strange bird. Bring your camera fast.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **take notes** --- write important information --- tomar apuntes --- *I always take notes in class.* --- *Siempre tomo apuntes en clase.* --- *She takes notes during every call. Nothing gets lost. The boss trusts her memory.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **take responsibility** --- accept that something is your duty or fault --- asumir responsabilidad --- *You need to take responsibility.* --- *Tenés que asumir la responsabilidad.* --- *He broke the window and took responsibility. He paid for the glass. His parents were proud.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **get ready** --- prepare yourself --- prepararse --- *Get ready for school.* --- *Preparate para la escuela.* --- *I suppose Dinah’ll be sending me on messages next!” And she began fancying the sort of thing that would happen: “‘Miss Alice! Come here directly, and get ready for your walk!’* --- *Alice's Adventures in Wonderland — Lewis Carroll*
- **get married** --- become someone's husband or wife --- casarse --- *They got married last year.* --- *Se casaron el año pasado.* --- *"But why?" cried Scrooge's nephew. "Why?" "Why did you get married?" said Scrooge.* --- *A Christmas Carol — Charles Dickens*
- **get better** --- improve --- mejorar --- *Your English is getting better.* --- *Tu inglés está mejorando.* --- *Peter did not hear him. He was begging Wendy to get better quickly, so that he could show her the mermaids.* --- *Peter Pan — J. M. Barrie*

## ADJECTIVE + NOUN
- **heavy rain** --- rain that falls hard --- lluvia intensa --- *We had heavy rain last night.* --- *Tuvimos lluvia intensa anoche.* --- *The heavy rain stopped the match. We waited one hour. Then we played in the mud.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **strong coffee** --- coffee with a powerful taste --- café fuerte --- *I like strong coffee.* --- *Me gusta el café fuerte.* --- *Grandpa drinks strong coffee daily. He makes it very dark. Nobody else can drink it.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **deep sleep** --- a very sound sleep --- sueño profundo --- *He was in a deep sleep.* --- *Estaba en un sueño profundo.* --- *The baby fell into a deep sleep. Don't make noise. She needs two hours.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **close friend** --- a good friend you know well --- amigo cercano --- *She's a close friend of mine.* --- *Es una amiga cercana mía.* --- *Tom is a close friend. He knows all my secrets. I trust him fully.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **high probability** --- a strong chance --- alta probabilidad --- *There's a high probability of rain.* --- *Hay una alta probabilidad de lluvia.* --- *There is a high probability of storms. Stay inside tonight. The radio warns everyone.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **serious problem** --- an important or difficult problem --- problema serio --- *We have a serious problem.* --- *Tenemos un problema serio.* --- *Late planes are a serious problem. We missed our connection. The company gave us a hotel.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **great opportunity** --- a very good chance --- gran oportunidad --- *This is a great opportunity.* --- *Esta es una gran oportunidad.* --- *The new job is a great opportunity. It pays well and it's near. Take it, Ana.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **hard work** --- work that requires a lot of effort --- trabajo duro --- *Success requires hard work.* --- *El éxito requiere trabajo duro.* --- *The garden needs hard work. We dig every weekend. The roses thank us.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **fast food** --- food prepared and served quickly --- comida rápida --- *We rarely eat fast food.* --- *Rara vez comemos comida rápida.* --- *They eat fast food every Friday. Burgers and fries. I prefer Grandma's soup.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **common mistake** --- an error many people make --- error común --- *That's a common mistake.* --- *Ese es un error común.* --- *Forgetting names is a common mistake. I do it always. Now I write them down.* --- *Everyday conversation*

## ADVERB + ADJECTIVE

- **deeply worried** --- very worried --- muy preocupado --- *She was deeply worried.* --- *Estaba muy preocupada.* --- *Mom looks deeply worried tonight. The test results arrive tomorrow. We hug her tight.* --- *Everyday conversation*

- **absolutely necessary** --- essential --- absolutamente necesario --- *Is that absolutely necessary?* --- *¿Eso es absolutamente necesario?* --- *A car is absolutely necessary here. There are no buses. We share one with neighbors.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **extremely difficult** --- very difficult --- extremadamente difícil --- *The exam was extremely difficult.* --- *El examen fue extremadamente difícil.* --- *Chinese is extremely difficult for me. The signs confuse me. But I keep trying.* --- *Everyday conversation*
