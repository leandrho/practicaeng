# Collocations esenciales B1--B2

Colección para tarjetas de práctica. Intentá completar o explicar la expresión antes de revelar la respuesta.

## MAKE
- **make a decision** --- choose what to do --- tomar una decisión --- *I need to make a decision today.* --- *Necesito tomar una decisión hoy.*
- **make a mistake** --- do something incorrectly --- cometer un error --- *Everyone makes mistakes.* --- *Todos cometemos errores.*
- **make an effort** --- try hard --- hacer un esfuerzo --- *You need to make an effort.* --- *Tenés que hacer un esfuerzo.*
- **make progress** --- improve or advance --- progresar --- *You're making good progress.* --- *Estás progresando mucho.*
- **make money** --- earn money --- ganar dinero --- *She makes money online.* --- *Ella gana dinero en línea.*
- **make a difference** --- have an important effect --- marcar una diferencia --- *Small changes can make a difference.* --- *Los cambios pequeños pueden marcar una diferencia.*
- **make a choice** --- choose between options --- hacer una elección --- *You have to make a choice.* --- *Tenés que hacer una elección.*
- **make a promise** --- say that you will do something --- hacer una promesa --- *I made a promise to my son.* --- *Le hice una promesa a mi hijo.*
- **make friends** --- become friends with people --- hacer amigos --- *It's easy to make friends there.* --- *Es fácil hacer amigos ahí.*
- **make plans** --- arrange future activities --- hacer planes --- *We're making plans for the weekend.* --- *Estamos haciendo planes para el fin de semana.*

## DO
- **do homework** --- complete schoolwork --- hacer la tarea --- *I have to do my homework.* --- *Tengo que hacer la tarea.*
- **do exercise** --- take physical exercise --- hacer ejercicio --- *I do exercise three times a week.* --- *Hago ejercicio tres veces por semana.*
- **do business** --- conduct commercial activity --- hacer negocios --- *We do business with several companies.* --- *Hacemos negocios con varias empresas.*
- **do your best** --- try as hard as possible --- hacer lo mejor posible --- *Just do your best.* --- *Simplemente hacé lo mejor que puedas.*
- **do someone a favor** --- do something helpful for someone --- hacerle un favor a alguien --- *Can you do me a favor?* --- *¿Me podés hacer un favor?*
- **do research** --- study a subject to discover information --- investigar --- *They're doing research on the topic.* --- *Están investigando el tema.*
- **do the shopping** --- buy food and household items --- hacer las compras --- *I'll do the shopping tonight.* --- *Voy a hacer las compras esta noche.*
- **do the dishes** --- wash dishes --- lavar los platos --- *I'll do the dishes.* --- *Voy a lavar los platos.*
- **do a good job** --- perform a task well --- hacer un buen trabajo --- *You did a good job.* --- *Hiciste un buen trabajo.*
- **do damage** --- cause harm --- causar daño --- *The storm did a lot of damage.* --- *La tormenta causó mucho daño.*

## HAVE / TAKE / GET
- **have a good time** --- enjoy yourself --- pasarla bien --- *We had a good time.* --- *La pasamos bien.*
- **have a conversation** --- talk with someone --- tener una conversación --- *We had a long conversation.* --- *Tuvimos una conversación larga.*
- **have a chance** --- have an opportunity --- tener una oportunidad --- *I finally have a chance to practice.* --- *Por fin tengo la oportunidad de practicar.*
- **take a break** --- stop working to rest --- tomarse un descanso --- *Let's take a break.* --- *Tomemos un descanso.*
- **take a look** --- look briefly --- echar un vistazo --- *Take a look at this.* --- *Echá un vistazo a esto.*
- **take notes** --- write important information --- tomar apuntes --- *I always take notes in class.* --- *Siempre tomo apuntes en clase.*
- **take responsibility** --- accept that something is your duty or fault --- asumir responsabilidad --- *You need to take responsibility.* --- *Tenés que asumir la responsabilidad.*
- **get ready** --- prepare yourself --- prepararse --- *Get ready for school.* --- *Preparate para la escuela.*
- **get married** --- become someone's husband or wife --- casarse --- *They got married last year.* --- *Se casaron el año pasado.*
- **get better** --- improve --- mejorar --- *Your English is getting better.* --- *Tu inglés está mejorando.*

## ADJECTIVE + NOUN
- **heavy rain** --- rain that falls hard --- lluvia intensa --- *We had heavy rain last night.* --- *Tuvimos lluvia intensa anoche.*
- **strong coffee** --- coffee with a powerful taste --- café fuerte --- *I like strong coffee.* --- *Me gusta el café fuerte.*
- **deep sleep** --- a very sound sleep --- sueño profundo --- *He was in a deep sleep.* --- *Estaba en un sueño profundo.*
- **close friend** --- a good friend you know well --- amigo cercano --- *She's a close friend of mine.* --- *Es una amiga cercana mía.*
- **high probability** --- a strong chance --- alta probabilidad --- *There's a high probability of rain.* --- *Hay una alta probabilidad de lluvia.*
- **serious problem** --- an important or difficult problem --- problema serio --- *We have a serious problem.* --- *Tenemos un problema serio.*
- **great opportunity** --- a very good chance --- gran oportunidad --- *This is a great opportunity.* --- *Esta es una gran oportunidad.*
- **hard work** --- work that requires a lot of effort --- trabajo duro --- *Success requires hard work.* --- *El éxito requiere trabajo duro.*
- **fast food** --- food prepared and served quickly --- comida rápida --- *We rarely eat fast food.* --- *Rara vez comemos comida rápida.*
- **common mistake** --- an error many people make --- error común --- *That's a common mistake.* --- *Ese es un error común.*

## ADVERB + ADJECTIVE
- **highly recommended** --- recommended very strongly --- muy recomendado --- *This book is highly recommended.* --- *Este libro es muy recomendado.*
- **deeply worried** --- very worried --- muy preocupado --- *She was deeply worried.* --- *Estaba muy preocupada.*
- **completely different** --- totally unlike --- completamente diferente --- *The two situations are completely different.* --- *Las dos situaciones son completamente diferentes.*
- **perfectly normal** --- entirely usual --- perfectamente normal --- *That's perfectly normal.* --- *Eso es perfectamente normal.*
- **fully aware** --- completely conscious of something --- plenamente consciente --- *I'm fully aware of the risks.* --- *Soy plenamente consciente de los riesgos.*
- **absolutely necessary** --- essential --- absolutamente necesario --- *Is that absolutely necessary?* --- *¿Eso es absolutamente necesario?*
- **extremely difficult** --- very difficult --- extremadamente difícil --- *The exam was extremely difficult.* --- *El examen fue extremadamente difícil.*
- **widely available** --- available in many places --- ampliamente disponible --- *The product is widely available.* --- *El producto está ampliamente disponible.*
- **closely related** --- strongly connected --- estrechamente relacionado --- *The two issues are closely related.* --- *Los dos temas están estrechamente relacionados.*
- **strongly recommended** --- advised with emphasis --- muy recomendado --- *Booking in advance is strongly recommended.* --- *Se recomienda mucho reservar con anticipación.*
