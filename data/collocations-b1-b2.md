# Collocations esenciales B1--B2

Colección para tarjetas de práctica. Intentá completar o explicar la
expresión antes de revelar la respuesta.

## MAKE

-   **make a decision** --- tomar una decisión --- *I need to make a
    decision today.*
-   **make a mistake** --- cometer un error --- *Everyone makes
    mistakes.*
-   **make an effort** --- hacer un esfuerzo --- *You need to make an
    effort.*
-   **make progress** --- progresar --- *You're making good progress.*
-   **make money** --- ganar dinero --- *She makes money online.*
-   **make a difference** --- marcar una diferencia --- *Small changes
    can make a difference.*
-   **make a choice** --- hacer una elección --- *You have to make a
    choice.*
-   **make a promise** --- hacer una promesa --- *I made a promise to my
    son.*
-   **make friends** --- hacer amigos --- *It's easy to make friends
    there.*
-   **make plans** --- hacer planes --- *We're making plans for the
    weekend.*

## DO

-   **do homework** --- hacer la tarea --- *I have to do my homework.*
-   **do exercise** --- hacer ejercicio --- *I do exercise three times a
    week.*
-   **do business** --- hacer negocios --- *We do business with several
    companies.*
-   **do your best** --- hacer lo mejor posible --- *Just do your best.*
-   **do someone a favor** --- hacerle un favor a alguien --- *Can you
    do me a favor?*
-   **do research** --- investigar --- *They're doing research on the
    topic.*
-   **do the shopping** --- hacer las compras --- *I'll do the shopping
    tonight.*
-   **do the dishes** --- lavar los platos --- *I'll do the dishes.*
-   **do a good job** --- hacer un buen trabajo --- *You did a good
    job.*
-   **do damage** --- causar daño --- *The storm did a lot of damage.*

## HAVE / TAKE / GET

-   **have a good time** --- pasarla bien --- *We had a good time.*
-   **have a conversation** --- tener una conversación --- *We had a
    long conversation.*
-   **have a chance** --- tener una oportunidad --- *I finally have a
    chance to practice.*
-   **take a break** --- tomarse un descanso --- *Let's take a break.*
-   **take a look** --- echar un vistazo --- *Take a look at this.*
-   **take notes** --- tomar apuntes --- *I always take notes in class.*
-   **take responsibility** --- asumir responsabilidad --- *You need to
    take responsibility.*
-   **get ready** --- prepararse --- *Get ready for school.*
-   **get married** --- casarse --- *They got married last year.*
-   **get better** --- mejorar --- *Your English is getting better.*

## ADJECTIVE + NOUN

-   **heavy rain** --- lluvia intensa --- *We had heavy rain last
    night.*
-   **strong coffee** --- café fuerte --- *I like strong coffee.*
-   **deep sleep** --- sueño profundo --- *He was in a deep sleep.*
-   **close friend** --- amigo cercano --- *She's a close friend of
    mine.*
-   **high probability** --- alta probabilidad --- *There's a high
    probability of rain.*
-   **serious problem** --- problema serio --- *We have a serious
    problem.*
-   **great opportunity** --- gran oportunidad --- *This is a great
    opportunity.*
-   **hard work** --- trabajo duro --- *Success requires hard work.*
-   **fast food** --- comida rápida --- *We rarely eat fast food.*
-   **common mistake** --- error común --- *That's a common mistake.*

## ADVERB + ADJECTIVE

-   **highly recommended** --- muy recomendado --- *This book is highly
    recommended.*
-   **deeply worried** --- muy preocupado --- *She was deeply worried.*
-   **completely different** --- completamente diferente --- *The two
    situations are completely different.*
-   **perfectly normal** --- perfectamente normal --- *That's perfectly
    normal.*
-   **fully aware** --- plenamente consciente --- *I'm fully aware of
    the risks.*
-   **absolutely necessary** --- absolutamente necesario --- *Is that
    absolutely necessary?*
-   **extremely difficult** --- extremadamente difícil --- *The exam was
    extremely difficult.*
-   **widely available** --- ampliamente disponible --- *The product is
    widely available.*
-   **closely related** --- estrechamente relacionado --- *The two
    issues are closely related.*
-   **strongly recommended** --- muy recomendado --- *Booking in advance
    is strongly recommended.*
