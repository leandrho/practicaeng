## Infinitives and the -ing form
### Regla y forma
- **EN:** Some verbs take **to + base verb**; others, the **-ing form**. After a preposition use **-ing**, and after a modal, the base verb without to.
- **ES:** Algunos verbos llevan **to + verbo base**; otros, **verbo en -ing**. Después de una preposición se usa **-ing** y después de un modal, verbo base sin to.
### Formación
- **Name (EN):** To-infinitive --- **Pattern:** Verb + to + base verb.
- **Name (EN):** -ing form --- **Pattern:** Verb or preposition + -ing form.
- **Name (EN):** After a modal --- **Pattern:** Modal + base verb (no to).
### Usos
- **EN:** Use to + verb to express purpose: I went out to buy milk. With can, should and other modals, use the base without to: can swim. Stop doing something means to quit doing it, while stop to do something means to interrupt another activity in order to do that. Remember to do something looks ahead to a pending task; remember doing something recalls a previous experience.
- **ES:** Usá to más verbo para expresar propósito: I went out to buy milk. Con can, should y otros modales se usa la base sin to: can swim. Stop doing something significa dejar de hacerlo, mientras que stop to do something significa detener otra actividad para hacer eso. Remember to do something mira hacia una tarea pendiente; remember doing something recuerda una experiencia anterior.
### Contrastes y errores
- **EN:** Enjoy to read is wrong: enjoy reading. After a preposition do not use the infinitive: good at cooking, not good at to cook. Need to leave expresses necessity; need leaving does not replace that construction. The change between stop smoking and stop to smoke alters the meaning of the sentence.
- **ES:** Enjoy to read es incorrecto: enjoy reading. Después de una preposición no uses el infinitivo: good at cooking, no good at to cook. Need to leave expresa necesidad; need leaving no sustituye esa construcción. El cambio entre stop smoking y stop to smoke altera el sentido de la oración.
### Ejemplos
- **EN:** He decided to apply for the course. --- **ES:** Él decidió anotarse en el curso.
- **EN:** We enjoy walking by the river. --- **ES:** Disfrutamos caminar junto al río.
- **EN:** She stopped to answer the phone. --- **ES:** Se detuvo para atender el teléfono.
### Ejercicios
- **Prompt (EN):** You made a decision yesterday: you will learn Italian. Express your decision using decide. --- **Model (EN):** I decided to learn Italian. --- **Explanation (ES):** *Decide* va seguido de infinitivo con *to* para expresar una decisión. --- **Translation (ES):** Decidí aprender italiano.
- **Prompt (EN):** Your friends like cooking together every weekend. Say what they enjoy. --- **Model (EN):** My friends enjoy cooking together every weekend. --- **Explanation (ES):** *Enjoy* lleva la forma *-ing* del verbo siguiente. --- **Translation (ES):** Mis amigos disfrutan cocinar juntos todos los fines de semana.
- **Prompt (EN):** You are good at playing chess. Say so using the preposition at. --- **Model (EN):** I am good at playing chess. --- **Explanation (ES):** Después de la preposición *at*, el verbo va en forma *-ing*. --- **Translation (ES):** Soy bueno jugando al ajedrez.
- **Prompt (EN):** You interrupted your walk in order to take a photo. Describe why you stopped. --- **Model (EN):** I stopped to take a photo. --- **Explanation (ES):** *Stop to take* significa detener una actividad para hacer otra. --- **Translation (ES):** Me detuve para sacar una foto.
- **Prompt (EN):** You have quit eating sweets at night. Express the habit you stopped with stop. --- **Model (EN):** I stopped eating sweets at night. --- **Explanation (ES):** *Stop* más *-ing* señala que se dejó de realizar esa actividad. --- **Translation (ES):** Dejé de comer dulces a la noche.

## Expressing possibility: may / might / would
### Regla y forma
- **EN:** **May/might + base verb** expresses possibility; **would + base verb** presents a conditioned result or a polite invitation. For a past possibility, use **may/might have + past participle**.
- **ES:** **May/might + verbo base** expresa posibilidad; **would + verbo base** presenta un resultado condicionado o una invitación cortés. Para una posibilidad pasada, usá **may/might have + participio pasado**.
### Formación
- **Name (EN):** May / might --- **Pattern:** Subject + may/might + base verb.
- **Name (EN):** Would (hypothetical condition) --- **Pattern:** Subject + would + base verb + condition.
- **Name (EN):** Past possibility --- **Pattern:** Subject + may/might have + past participle.
### Usos
- **EN:** Use may or might when you are not certain about a present or future fact; might can sound somewhat more tentative, but the difference is not always fixed. Use would to imagine what would happen in a hypothetical situation or for a polite invitation, like Would you like some tea? With have + participle, may or might let you talk about past possibilities.
- **ES:** Usá may o might cuando no tenés certeza sobre un hecho presente o futuro; might puede sonar algo más tentativo, pero la diferencia no siempre es fija. Usá would para imaginar qué pasaría en una situación hipotética o para una invitación cortés, como Would you like some tea? Con have más participio, may o might permiten hablar de posibilidades pasadas.
### Contrastes y errores
- **EN:** He may come expresses uncertainty about whether he comes; He would come if he could presents a conditioned consequence. After all three modals goes the base: might arrive, not might arrives. For an uncertain past fact say might have forgotten, not might forgot. Do not confuse would with will when expressing a sure plan.
- **ES:** He may come expresa incertidumbre sobre si viene; He would come if he could presenta una consecuencia condicionada. Después de los tres modales va la base: might arrive, no might arrives. Para un hecho pasado incierto se dice might have forgotten, no might forgot. No confundas would con will al expresar un plan seguro.
### Ejemplos
- **EN:** We might visit our cousins this weekend. --- **ES:** Quizá visitemos a nuestros primos este fin de semana.
- **EN:** The delivery may be delayed by the storm. --- **ES:** Puede que la entrega se retrase por la tormenta.
- **EN:** I would help you if I had more time. --- **ES:** Te ayudaría si tuviera más tiempo.
### Ejercicios
- **Prompt (EN):** You are not sure whether the shop is open today. Express the possibility with may. --- **Model (EN):** The shop may be open today. --- **Explanation (ES):** *May* más verbo base indica que un hecho es posible, sin afirmarlo con certeza. --- **Translation (ES):** Puede que el negocio esté abierto hoy.
- **Prompt (EN):** Your friends have not confirmed the trip. Say they possibly will not go using might not. --- **Model (EN):** My friends might not go on the trip. --- **Explanation (ES):** *Might not* expresa la posibilidad de que la acción no ocurra. --- **Translation (ES):** Puede que mis amigos no vayan al viaje.
- **Prompt (EN):** You cannot afford a new laptop now. Imagine what you would buy if you had more money. --- **Model (EN):** I would buy a new laptop if I had more money. --- **Explanation (ES):** *Would* expresa una consecuencia hipotética condicionada por tener más dinero. --- **Translation (ES):** Compraría una computadora portátil nueva si tuviera más plata.
- **Prompt (EN):** Your neighbor did not answer the door yesterday. Suggest that they possibly went out, using might have. --- **Model (EN):** My neighbor might have gone out yesterday. --- **Explanation (ES):** *Might have* más participio expresa una posibilidad sobre un hecho pasado. --- **Translation (ES):** Puede que mi vecino haya salido ayer.
- **Prompt (EN):** Offer your guest a cup of coffee politely, using would like. --- **Model (EN):** Would you like a cup of coffee? --- **Explanation (ES):** *Would you like* forma una oferta cortés; no expresa incertidumbre factual como *may*. --- **Translation (ES):** ¿Te gustaría tomar una taza de café?

## Making deductions: must / can’t
### Regla y forma
- **EN:** **Must/can't + base verb** lets you deduce about the present; to deduce about the past, use **must/can't have + past participle**. Mustn't expresses prohibition, not a negative deduction.
- **ES:** **Must/can't + verbo base** permite deducir sobre el presente; para deducir sobre el pasado, se usa **must/can't have + participio pasado**. Mustn't expresa prohibición, no una deducción negativa.
### Formación
- **Name (EN):** Present deduction --- **Pattern:** Subject + must/can't + base verb (or be + adjective).
- **Name (EN):** Past deduction --- **Pattern:** Subject + must/can't have + past participle.
### Usos
- **EN:** Use must when the evidence strongly favours one explanation, and can't when the evidence rules a possibility out. The context supports the deduction: if all the lights are off, the office must be closed; if you saw someone leave, that person can't still be inside. With past facts, the participle after have places the hypothesis before the present.
- **ES:** Usá must cuando la evidencia favorece fuertemente una explicación, y can't cuando la evidencia descarta una posibilidad. El contexto sostiene la deducción: si todas las luces están apagadas, la oficina must be closed; si viste salir a alguien, esa persona can't still be inside. Con hechos pasados, el participio después de have sitúa la hipótesis antes del presente.
### Contrastes y errores
- **EN:** Mustn't expresses prohibition, not the negative conclusion that something is impossible; for that conclusion use can't. She must have left indicates an inference about the past, not a past obligation. Do not say can't has gone: after can't use have. Weak evidence usually justifies may or might, not a strong deduction with must.
- **ES:** Mustn't expresa prohibición, no la conclusión negativa de que algo es imposible; para esa conclusión se usa can't. She must have left indica una inferencia sobre el pasado, no una obligación pasada. No digas can't has gone: después de can't se usa have. Una prueba débil suele justificar may o might, no una deducción fuerte con must.
### Ejemplos
- **EN:** His office light is on; he must still be working. --- **ES:** La luz de su oficina está encendida; seguramente todavía está trabajando.
- **EN:** This key can't be hers; she left hers at home. --- **ES:** Esta llave no puede ser de ella; dejó la suya en casa.
- **EN:** The floor is wet; it must have rained overnight. --- **ES:** El piso está mojado; seguramente llovió durante la noche.
### Ejercicios
- **Prompt (EN):** Your friend has been running for an hour and is out of breath. Make a strong deduction about how they feel with must. --- **Model (EN):** My friend must be tired. --- **Explanation (ES):** *Must* expresa una conclusión probable apoyada en la evidencia física. --- **Translation (ES):** Mi amigo debe de estar cansado.
- **Prompt (EN):** Someone says the museum is open, but you can see a sign saying it is closed today. Reject that conclusion with can't. --- **Model (EN):** The museum can't be open today. --- **Explanation (ES):** *Can't* expresa una deducción negativa basada en un indicio claro. --- **Translation (ES):** El museo no puede estar abierto hoy.
- **Prompt (EN):** You find the cake plate empty, and your brother has crumbs on his shirt. Deduce what he did with must have. --- **Model (EN):** My brother must have eaten the cake. --- **Explanation (ES):** *Must have* más participio presenta una conclusión fuerte sobre un hecho pasado. --- **Translation (ES):** Mi hermano debe de haberse comido la torta.
- **Prompt (EN):** Ana left town on Monday. Someone claims they saw her here on Tuesday, but you know she was abroad. Make a past deduction with can't have. --- **Model (EN):** Ana can't have been here on Tuesday. --- **Explanation (ES):** *Can't have* más participio descarta un hecho pasado incompatible con la evidencia. --- **Translation (ES):** Ana no puede haber estado acá el martes.
- **Prompt (EN):** The meeting room is dark and the door is locked. Make a strong deduction about the meeting with must. --- **Model (EN):** The meeting must be over. --- **Explanation (ES):** *Must* más verbo base indica una conclusión muy probable a partir de las señales presentes. --- **Translation (ES):** La reunión debe de haber terminado.

## Question tags
### Regla y forma
- **EN:** The **question tag** repeats the auxiliary or modal with the opposite polarity and a subject pronoun. If the sentence has no auxiliary, the tag uses **do/does/did**.
- **ES:** La **question tag** repite el auxiliar o modal con polaridad opuesta y un pronombre sujeto. Si la oración no tiene auxiliar, la tag usa **do/does/did**.
### Formación
- **Name (EN):** Question tag --- **Pattern:** Statement + auxiliary/modal with opposite polarity + subject pronoun. No auxiliary: do/does/did + pronoun.
### Usos
- **EN:** If the main sentence has be, have as an auxiliary or a modal, repeat that auxiliary in the tag. If it has no auxiliary, use do, does or did according to the tense. For the imperative, will you? is common; with Let's, use shall we? A rising intonation usually expresses a genuine question and a falling one seeks confirmation.
- **ES:** Si la oración principal tiene be, have como auxiliar o un modal, repetí ese auxiliar en la tag. Si no tiene auxiliar, usá do, does o did según el tiempo verbal. Para el imperativo, will you? es frecuente; con Let's, se usa shall we? La entonación ascendente suele expresar una pregunta genuina y la descendente busca confirmación.
### Contrastes y errores
- **EN:** With he works, the tag is doesn't he?, not isn't he?, because the main verb is not be. With a negative like She didn't call, the tag turns positive: did she? The usual tag for I am late is aren't I?; not amn't I? in general standard English. Do not repeat the noun in the tag: use a pronoun.
- **ES:** Con he works, la tag es doesn't he?, no isn't he?, porque el verbo principal no es be. Con una negativa como She didn't call, la tag pasa a positiva: did she? La tag habitual de I am late es aren't I?; no amn't I? en inglés estándar general. No repitas el sustantivo en la tag: usá un pronombre.
### Ejemplos
- **EN:** Your sister lives nearby, doesn't she? --- **ES:** Tu hermana vive cerca, ¿no?
- **EN:** They weren't at the party, were they? --- **ES:** No estuvieron en la fiesta, ¿verdad?
- **EN:** Let's sit outside, shall we? --- **ES:** Sentémonos afuera, ¿dale?
### Ejercicios
- **Prompt (EN):** You believe your colleague is ready. Ask for confirmation by adding a question tag to She is ready. --- **Model (EN):** She is ready, isn't she? --- **Explanation (ES):** La oración afirmativa con *is* lleva *tag* negativa con el mismo auxiliar. --- **Translation (ES):** Ella está lista, ¿no?
- **Prompt (EN):** Your classmates did not finish the task. Check that fact by adding a positive tag. --- **Model (EN):** They didn't finish the task, did they? --- **Explanation (ES):** Una oración negativa en *past simple* lleva *tag* afirmativa con *did*. --- **Translation (ES):** No terminaron la tarea, ¿verdad?
- **Prompt (EN):** You expect your friend can swim. Ask for confirmation with a tag using can. --- **Model (EN):** You can swim, can't you? --- **Explanation (ES):** El modal *can* de la oración se repite en forma negativa en la *tag*. --- **Translation (ES):** Sabés nadar, ¿no?
- **Prompt (EN):** You say that you are early, but want to check. Add the usual tag for I am early. --- **Model (EN):** I am early, aren't I? --- **Explanation (ES):** Para *I am*, la *tag* negativa usual en inglés estándar general es *aren't I*? --- **Translation (ES):** Llegué temprano, ¿no?
- **Prompt (EN):** Suggest that you and your friend start now. Add the conventional tag after Let's start now. --- **Model (EN):** Let's start now, shall we? --- **Explanation (ES):** Después de *Let's* se usa habitualmente *shall we*? para pedir acuerdo. --- **Translation (ES):** Empecemos ahora, ¿dale?
