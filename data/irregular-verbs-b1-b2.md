# Verbos irregulares esenciales B1--B2

Colección para tarjetas de práctica. Mirá la forma base con pasado y participio, intentá recordar el significado y crear tu propio ejemplo antes de revelar la respuesta.

## A–B

- **be – was/were – been** --- exist or be present --- ser / estar --- *I was tired yesterday.* --- *Ayer estaba cansado.*
- **become – became – become** --- start to be something --- volverse / llegar a ser --- *She became a doctor last year.* --- *Se recibió de médica el año pasado.*
- **begin – began – begun** --- start --- empezar --- *The movie began late.* --- *La película empezó tarde.*
- **bend – bent – bent** --- curve or lean --- doblar / inclinarse --- *He bent the wire.* --- *Dobló el alambre.*
- **bite – bit – bitten** --- cut with teeth --- morder --- *The dog bit the postman.* --- *El perro mordió al cartero.*
- **bleed – bled – bled** --- lose blood --- sangrar --- *His nose bled after the game.* --- *Le sangró la nariz después del partido.*
- **blow – blew – blown** --- move air --- soplar --- *The wind blew all night.* --- *El viento sopló toda la noche.*
- **break – broke – broken** --- separate into pieces --- romper --- *I broke my phone yesterday.* --- *Rompí mi teléfono ayer.*
- **bring – brought – brought** --- carry here --- traer --- *She brought coffee for everyone.* --- *Trajo café para todos.*
- **build – built – built** --- construct --- construir --- *They built a new school.* --- *Construyeron una escuela nueva.*
- **burn – burnt – burnt** --- be on fire --- quemar / arder --- *I burnt the toast this morning.* --- *Quemé las tostadas esta mañana.*
- **buy – bought – bought** --- get with money --- comprar --- *We bought a new car.* --- *Compramos un auto nuevo.*

## C–D

- **catch – caught – caught** --- take hold of --- atrapar --- *I caught the bus just in time.* --- *Alcancé el colectivo justo a tiempo.*
- **choose – chose – chosen** --- pick --- elegir --- *She chose the red dress.* --- *Eligió el vestido rojo.*
- **come – came – come** --- move here --- venir --- *They came late to class.* --- *Llegaron tarde a clase.*
- **cost – cost – cost** --- have a price --- costar --- *This book cost ten dollars.* --- *Este libro costó diez dólares.*
- **cut – cut – cut** --- divide with a knife --- cortar --- *He cut his finger.* --- *Se cortó el dedo.*
- **dig – dug – dug** --- make a hole --- cavar --- *They dug a hole in the garden.* --- *Cavaron un pozo en el jardín.*
- **do – did – done** --- perform --- hacer --- *I did my homework last night.* --- *Hice mi tarea anoche.*
- **draw – drew – drawn** --- make a picture --- dibujar --- *She drew a beautiful landscape.* --- *Dibujó un paisaje hermoso.*
- **drink – drank – drunk** --- swallow liquid --- tomar / beber --- *He drank too much coffee.* --- *Tomó demasiado café.*
- **drive – drove – driven** --- control a car --- manejar --- *My father drove us to school.* --- *Mi papá nos llevó a la escuela en auto.*

## E–H

- **eat – ate – eaten** --- consume food --- comer --- *We ate pizza last night.* --- *Comimos pizza anoche.*
- **fall – fell – fallen** --- drop down --- caerse --- *He fell off his bike.* --- *Se cayó de la bici.*
- **feed – fed – fed** --- give food --- alimentar --- *She fed the baby at noon.* --- *Le dio de comer al bebé al mediodía.*
- **feel – felt – felt** --- have a sensation --- sentir / sentirse --- *I felt sick yesterday.* --- *Me sentí mal ayer.*
- **fight – fought – fought** --- struggle against --- pelear --- *They fought for their rights.* --- *Pelearon por sus derechos.*
- **find – found – found** --- discover --- encontrar --- *I found my keys under the table.* --- *Encontré mis llaves bajo la mesa.*
- **fly – flew – flown** --- travel by air --- volar --- *We flew to Madrid.* --- *Volamos a Madrid.*
- **forget – forgot – forgotten** --- fail to remember --- olvidar --- *I forgot your birthday.* --- *Me olvidé de tu cumpleaños.*
- **forgive – forgave – forgiven** --- stop blaming --- perdonar --- *She forgave him after the argument.* --- *Lo perdonó después de la discusión.*
- **freeze – froze – frozen** --- turn to ice --- congelar --- *The pipes froze last winter.* --- *Las cañerías se congelaron el invierno pasado.*
- **get – got – gotten** --- obtain --- conseguir / obtener --- *I got a new job.* --- *Conseguí un trabajo nuevo.*
- **give – gave – given** --- hand over --- dar --- *He gave me a nice gift.* --- *Me dio un lindo regalo.*
- **go – went – gone** --- move or travel --- ir --- *We went to the beach.* --- *Fuimos a la playa.*
- **grow – grew – grown** --- increase --- crecer / cultivar --- *My son grew a lot this year.* --- *Mi hijo creció mucho este año.*
- **hang – hung – hung** --- suspend --- colgar --- *She hung the picture on the wall.* --- *Colgó el cuadro en la pared.*
- **have – had – had** --- possess --- tener --- *We had a great time.* --- *La pasamos muy bien.*
- **hear – heard – heard** --- perceive sound --- escuchar / oír --- *I heard a strange noise.* --- *Escuché un ruido extraño.*
- **hide – hid – hidden** --- conceal --- esconder --- *The kids hid behind the door.* --- *Los chicos se escondieron detrás de la puerta.*
- **hit – hit – hit** --- strike --- golpear --- *He hit the ball hard.* --- *Golpeó fuerte la pelota.*
- **hold – held – held** --- grasp --- sostener --- *She held my hand.* --- *Me sostuvo la mano.*
- **hurt – hurt – hurt** --- cause pain --- lastimar / doler --- *My leg hurt after running.* --- *Me dolía la pierna después de correr.*

## K–M

- **keep – kept – kept** --- retain --- guardar / mantener --- *Keep the receipt for the warranty.* --- *Guardá el ticket para la garantía.*
- **know – knew – known** --- have knowledge --- saber / conocer --- *I knew the answer.* --- *Sabía la respuesta.*
- **lay – laid – laid** --- put down --- poner / colocar --- *She laid the baby on the bed.* --- *Acostó al bebé en la cama.*
- **lead – led – led** --- guide --- liderar / guiar --- *He led the team to victory.* --- *Guió al equipo a la victoria.*
- **leave – left – left** --- go away --- irse / dejar --- *We left early this morning.* --- *Nos fuimos temprano esta mañana.*
- **lend – lent – lent** --- give temporarily --- prestar --- *Can you lend me your pen?* --- *¿Me prestás tu lapicera?*
- **let – let – let** --- allow --- dejar / permitir --- *They let us enter for free.* --- *Nos dejaron entrar gratis.*
- **lie – lay – lain** --- rest horizontally --- recostarse / yacer --- *He lay on the sofa all afternoon.* --- *Estuvo recostado en el sillón toda la tarde.*
- **light – lit – lit** --- illuminate --- encender / iluminar --- *She lit a candle.* --- *Encendió una vela.*
- **lose – lost – lost** --- misplace --- perder --- *I lost my wallet.* --- *Perdí mi billetera.*
- **make – made – made** --- create --- hacer / fabricar --- *They made a cake for the party.* --- *Hicieron una torta para la fiesta.*
- **mean – meant – meant** --- signify --- significar --- *What did you mean by that?* --- *¿Qué quisiste decir con eso?*
- **meet – met – met** --- encounter --- conocer / encontrarse --- *I met him in 2020.* --- *Lo conocí en 2020.*

## P–R

- **pay – paid – paid** --- give money --- pagar --- *She paid the bill yesterday.* --- *Pagó la cuenta ayer.*
- **put – put – put** --- place --- poner --- *Put your coat on the chair.* --- *Poné tu campera en la silla.*
- **quit – quit – quit** --- leave or stop --- dejar / renunciar --- *He quit his job in June.* --- *Renunció a su trabajo en junio.*
- **read – read – read** --- look at words --- leer --- *I read three books last month.* --- *Leí tres libros el mes pasado.*
- **ride – rode – ridden** --- travel on --- andar / montar --- *She rode her bike to work.* --- *Fue al trabajo en bici.*
- **ring – rang – rung** --- make a bell sound --- sonar / llamar --- *The phone rang twice.* --- *El teléfono sonó dos veces.*
- **rise – rose – risen** --- go up --- subir / elevarse --- *The sun rose at six.* --- *El sol salió a las seis.*
- **run – ran – run** --- move fast --- correr --- *He ran five kilometers.* --- *Corrió cinco kilómetros.*

## S

- **say – said – said** --- speak words --- decir --- *She said hello to everyone.* --- *Saludó a todos.*
- **see – saw – seen** --- perceive with eyes --- ver --- *I saw a great film.* --- *Vi una gran película.*
- **sell – sold – sold** --- exchange for money --- vender --- *They sold their old house.* --- *Vendieron su casa vieja.*
- **send – sent – sent** --- dispatch --- enviar --- *I sent you an email.* --- *Te envié un mail.*
- **set – set – set** --- put or adjust --- poner / ajustar --- *She set the table for dinner.* --- *Puso la mesa para la cena.*
- **shake – shook – shaken** --- move quickly --- sacudir --- *He shook my hand.* --- *Me dio la mano.*
- **shine – shone – shone** --- give light --- brillar --- *The sun shone all day.* --- *El sol brilló todo el día.*
- **shoot – shot – shot** --- fire --- disparar --- *They shot the scene twice.* --- *Grabaron la escena dos veces.*
- **show – showed – shown** --- display --- mostrar --- *He showed us his photos.* --- *Nos mostró sus fotos.*
- **shut – shut – shut** --- close --- cerrar --- *Shut the door, please.* --- *Cerrá la puerta, por favor.*
- **sing – sang – sung** --- make music with voice --- cantar --- *She sang a sad song.* --- *Cantó una canción triste.*
- **sink – sank – sunk** --- go under water --- hundirse --- *The boat sank quickly.* --- *El barco se hundió rápido.*
- **sit – sat – sat** --- take a seat --- sentarse --- *We sat near the window.* --- *Nos sentamos cerca de la ventana.*
- **sleep – slept – slept** --- rest at night --- dormir --- *I slept eight hours.* --- *Dormí ocho horas.*
- **slide – slid – slid** --- glide --- deslizarse --- *The box slid across the floor.* --- *La caja se deslizó por el piso.*
- **speak – spoke – spoken** --- talk --- hablar --- *She spoke three languages.* --- *Hablaba tres idiomas.*
- **spend – spent – spent** --- use money or time --- gastar / pasar --- *We spent the weekend at home.* --- *Pasamos el fin de semana en casa.*
- **split – split – split** --- divide --- dividir --- *They split the bill.* --- *Dividieron la cuenta.*
- **stand – stood – stood** --- be upright --- estar de pie --- *We stood in line for an hour.* --- *Hicimos cola parados una hora.*
- **steal – stole – stolen** --- take without permission --- robar --- *Someone stole my bike.* --- *Alguien me robó la bici.*
- **stick – stuck – stuck** --- attach --- pegar --- *I stuck the note on the fridge.* --- *Pegué la nota en la heladera.*
- **strike – struck – struck** --- hit --- golpear --- *Lightning struck the tree.* --- *Un rayo golpeó el árbol.*
- **swear – swore – sworn** --- promise --- jurar --- *He swore to tell the truth.* --- *Juró decir la verdad.*
- **sweep – swept – swept** --- clean with broom --- barrer --- *She swept the kitchen.* --- *Barrió la cocina.*
- **swim – swam – swum** --- move in water --- nadar --- *We swam in the lake.* --- *Nadamos en el lago.*

## T–W

- **take – took – taken** --- carry or remove --- llevar / tomar --- *She took a photo of us.* --- *Nos sacó una foto.*
- **teach – taught – taught** --- give lessons --- enseñar --- *He taught me to swim.* --- *Me enseñó a nadar.*
- **tear – tore – torn** --- rip --- rasgar --- *I tore my jeans.* --- *Me rasgué el jean.*
- **tell – told – told** --- inform --- contar / decir --- *She told me the truth.* --- *Me dijo la verdad.*
- **think – thought – thought** --- have an opinion --- pensar --- *I thought about you yesterday.* --- *Pensé en vos ayer.*
- **throw – threw – thrown** --- launch --- tirar / lanzar --- *He threw the ball to me.* --- *Me tiró la pelota.*
- **understand – understood – understood** --- comprehend --- entender --- *I finally understood the lesson.* --- *Por fin entendí la lección.*
- **wake – woke – woken** --- stop sleeping --- despertar --- *I woke up at seven.* --- *Me desperté a las siete.*
- **wear – wore – worn** --- have clothes on --- usar / vestir --- *She wore a blue dress.* --- *Usó un vestido azul.*
