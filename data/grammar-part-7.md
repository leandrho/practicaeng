## Reported Speech: statements, special introductory verbs, questions, commands and requests
### Regla y forma
- **EN:** In reported speech, **said (that) + clause** or **told + person + clause** reports statements; **asked + person + if/whether or wh-word + clause** reports questions. For orders and requests use **told/asked + person + (not) to + base verb**.
- **ES:** En reported speech, **said (that) + oración** o **told + persona + oración** reporta afirmaciones; **asked + persona + if/whether o palabra interrogativa + oración** reporta preguntas. Para pedidos y órdenes se usa **told/asked + persona + (not) to + verbo base**.
### Formación
- **Name (EN):** Statements --- **Pattern:** Said (that) + clause / told + person + clause.
- **Name (EN):** Questions --- **Pattern:** Asked + person + if/whether or wh-word + clause in statement order.
- **Name (EN):** Commands and requests --- **Pattern:** Told/asked + person + (not) to + base verb.
### Usos
- **EN:** For statements, use said (that) + clause or told + person + clause. For yes/no questions, use asked + person + if or whether; for open questions, keep the wh-word, but use statement order. Orders and requests use told or asked + person + to + base verb; for prohibitions, not to. Verbs like advise, suggest and promise allow nuances: advise someone to do something, suggest doing something and promise to do something.
- **ES:** Para afirmaciones, usá said (that) más cláusula o told más persona más cláusula. Para preguntas de respuesta sí/no, usá asked más persona más if o whether; para preguntas abiertas, conservá la palabra interrogativa, pero usá orden afirmativo. Las órdenes y los pedidos usan told o asked más persona más to y verbo base; para prohibiciones, not to. Verbos como advise, suggest y promise permiten matices: advise someone to do something, suggest doing something y promise to do something.
### Contrastes y errores
- **EN:** Do not use question order inside the subordinate clause: She asked where I lived, not where did I live. Said does not take a personal object directly in this pattern: she told me, but not she said to me. Suggest does not follow the pattern suggest me to go; say suggested going or suggested that we go. When a statement is still true, the tense change may not be needed, but keep consistency with the context.
- **ES:** No uses orden de pregunta dentro de la subordinada: She asked where I lived, no where did I live. Said no toma directamente un objeto personal en este patrón: she told me, pero she said to me. Suggest no sigue el patrón suggest me to go; decí suggested going o suggested that we go. Cuando una afirmación sigue siendo cierta, el cambio de tiempo puede no ser necesario, pero mantené coherencia con el contexto.
### Ejemplos
- **EN:** Leo said that he was busy that day. --- **ES:** Leo dijo que estaba ocupado ese día.
- **EN:** The teacher asked us if we had finished the task. --- **ES:** La profesora nos preguntó si habíamos terminado la tarea.
- **EN:** The guide told us not to touch the paintings. --- **ES:** El guía nos dijo que no tocáramos los cuadros.
### Ejercicios
- **Prompt (EN):** Yesterday, Emma said, "I am tired today." Report her statement today using said and the appropriate time reference. --- **Model (EN):** Emma said that she was tired that day. --- **Explanation (ES):** Con *said* en pasado, *am* pasa a *was*; *I* cambia a *she* y *today* a *that day*. --- **Translation (ES):** Emma dijo que estaba cansada ese día.
- **Prompt (EN):** Your manager said to you, "I advise you to check the figures." Report the advice with the introductory verb advised. --- **Model (EN):** My manager advised me to check the figures. --- **Explanation (ES):** *Advise* lleva persona más infinitivo con *to* para transmitir un consejo. --- **Translation (ES):** Mi encargado me aconsejó revisar las cifras.
- **Prompt (EN):** A friend asked you, "Where do you live?" Report the question using asked. --- **Model (EN):** My friend asked me where I lived. --- **Explanation (ES):** Una pregunta indirecta mantiene *where* pero usa orden afirmativo y, con *asked* en pasado, *lived*. --- **Translation (ES):** Mi amigo me preguntó dónde vivía.
- **Prompt (EN):** The coach said to the players, "Don't stop running." Report the negative command with told. --- **Model (EN):** The coach told the players not to stop running. --- **Explanation (ES):** Una orden negativa se transmite con *told* más persona más *not to* y verbo base. --- **Translation (ES):** El entrenador les dijo a los jugadores que no dejaran de correr.
- **Prompt (EN):** The receptionist said to you, "Please wait in the lobby." Report the polite request with asked. --- **Model (EN):** The receptionist asked me to wait in the lobby. --- **Explanation (ES):** Un pedido se transmite con *asked* más persona más *to* y verbo base. --- **Translation (ES):** La recepcionista me pidió que esperara en la recepción.

## Clauses of result
### Regla y forma
- **EN:** **So + adjective/adverb + that + clause** and **such + (a/an) + adjective + noun + that + clause** express intensity and result. **Too + adjective + to + verb** expresses excess; **adjective + enough + to + verb**, sufficient degree.
- **ES:** **So + adjetivo/adverbio + that + oración** y **such + (a/an) + adjetivo + sustantivo + that + oración** expresan intensidad y resultado. **Too + adjetivo + to + verbo** expresa exceso; **adjetivo + enough + to + verbo**, grado suficiente.
### Formación
- **Name (EN):** So ... that --- **Pattern:** So + adjective/adverb + that + clause.
- **Name (EN):** Such ... that --- **Pattern:** Such + (a/an) + adjective + noun + that + clause.
- **Name (EN):** Too ... to --- **Pattern:** Too + adjective + to + base verb.
- **Name (EN):** ... enough to --- **Pattern:** Adjective + enough + to + base verb.
### Usos
- **EN:** Use so ... that to measure the intensity of a quality and such ... that when the central expression is a noun. With a singular countable noun, such takes a or an: such a difficult task. With plurals and uncountables, use such without a or an. Too usually marks an excess that prevents an action; enough says the degree is sufficient to perform it.
- **ES:** Usá so ... that para medir la intensidad de una cualidad y such ... that cuando la expresión central es un sustantivo. Con un sustantivo contable singular, such lleva a o an: such a difficult task. Con plurales e incontables, se usa such sin a o an. Too suele indicar un exceso que impide una acción; enough señala que el grado alcanza para realizarla.
### Contrastes y errores
- **EN:** It's so interesting a book that ... does not follow the common pattern of this unit: say It's such an interesting book that ... . After so goes an adjective or adverb; after such goes a noun phrase. Do not add that after too or enough if you continue with an infinitive: too tired to go, not too tired that to go. The consequence must be stated, not merely hinted at.
- **ES:** It's so interesting a book that ... no sigue el patrón común de esta unidad: decí It's such an interesting book that ... . Después de so va un adjetivo o adverbio; después de such va un grupo nominal. No agregues that después de too o enough si seguís con un infinitivo: too tired to go, no too tired that to go. La consecuencia debe estar expresada, no solamente insinuada.
### Ejemplos
- **EN:** The puzzle was so hard that nobody solved it. --- **ES:** El rompecabezas era tan difícil que nadie lo resolvió.
- **EN:** It was such a warm evening that we ate outside. --- **ES:** Era una noche tan cálida que cenamos afuera.
- **EN:** The shelf is too high for me to reach. --- **ES:** El estante está demasiado alto para que yo lo alcance.
### Ejercicios
- **Prompt (EN):** The lecture was very interesting, and everyone stayed until the end. Connect intensity and result with so ... that. --- **Model (EN):** The lecture was so interesting that everyone stayed until the end. --- **Explanation (ES):** *So* más adjetivo más *that* une la intensidad de *interesting* con su consecuencia. --- **Translation (ES):** La charla fue tan interesante que todos se quedaron hasta el final.
- **Prompt (EN):** The storm was very strong, and all flights were cancelled. Express the result with such a ... that. --- **Model (EN):** It was such a strong storm that all flights were cancelled. --- **Explanation (ES):** *Such a* más adjetivo y sustantivo singular introduce la causa de la consecuencia. --- **Translation (ES):** Fue una tormenta tan fuerte que se cancelaron todos los vuelos.
- **Prompt (EN):** There was a lot of traffic, so you arrived late. Use such ... that with the uncountable noun traffic. --- **Model (EN):** There was such heavy traffic that I arrived late. --- **Explanation (ES):** *Traffic* es incontable; *such* más adjetivo y sustantivo va sin *a* o *an*. --- **Translation (ES):** Había tanto tránsito que llegué tarde.
- **Prompt (EN):** The box weighs too much for your sister to lift. Express the result with too ... to. --- **Model (EN):** The box is too heavy for my sister to lift. --- **Explanation (ES):** *Too* más adjetivo más *to* expresa un exceso que impide la acción. --- **Translation (ES):** La caja es demasiado pesada para que mi hermana la levante.
- **Prompt (EN):** Your brother is now tall enough to reach the top shelf. Express the possible result with enough ... to. --- **Model (EN):** My brother is tall enough to reach the top shelf. --- **Explanation (ES):** *Enough* después del adjetivo indica que se alcanza la altura necesaria para la acción con *to*. --- **Translation (ES):** Mi hermano es lo suficientemente alto como para alcanzar el estante de arriba.
