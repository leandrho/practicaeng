## Past Simple / Past Progressive
### Regla y forma
- **EN:** The **past simple** uses the past form of the verb and **did + base verb** for negatives and questions. The **past progressive** combines **was/were + the -ing form** for an action in progress in the past.
- **ES:** El **past simple** usa la forma pasada del verbo y **did + verbo base** para negativas y preguntas. El **past progressive** combina **was/were + verbo en -ing** para una acción en desarrollo en el pasado.
### Formación
- **Name (EN):** Past Simple
  - **Affirmative:** subject + past form of the verb
  - **Negative:** subject + didn't + base verb
  - **Question:** Did + subject + base verb?
- **Name (EN):** Past Progressive
  - **Affirmative:** subject + was/were + -ing form
  - **Negative:** subject + was/were + not + -ing form
  - **Question:** Was/were + subject + -ing form?
### Definición
- **EN:** The **past simple** presents a past event as complete, even if it lasted a long time; successive simple verbs move a story forward (I arrived, sat down and called her). The **past progressive** places you inside an activity at a particular past moment, without saying when it began or ended (I was reading at eight). Together, the progressive can set the scene while a shorter simple event interrupts it: I was reading when she called.
- **ES:** El **past simple** presenta un hecho pasado como terminado, aunque haya durado mucho; varios verbos en simple hacen avanzar un relato (I arrived, sat down and called her). El **past progressive** te sitúa dentro de una actividad en un momento pasado, sin indicar cuándo empezó o terminó (I was reading at eight). Juntos, el progressive puede dar el contexto mientras un hecho más breve en simple lo interrumpe: I was reading when she called.
### Usos
- **EN:** Use the past simple for actions finished at a moment in the past or for a sequence of facts. Use the past progressive for an action in progress at a moment in the past or as the background of another action. When usually introduces the event that interrupts; while can introduce the activity that was already in progress.
- **ES:** Usá past simple para acciones terminadas en un momento pasado o para una secuencia de hechos. Usá past progressive para una acción en curso en un momento del pasado o como trasfondo de otra acción. When suele introducir el suceso que interrumpe; while puede introducir la actividad que ya estaba en progreso.
### Contrastes y errores
- **EN:** In a question with did, do not repeat the past form: Did you go?, not Did you went?. A short action that interrupts another usually takes the simple: I was reading when the lights went out. Was or were do not combine with the past form to build a progressive: were walking, not were walked.
- **ES:** En una pregunta con did no repitas la forma pasada: Did you go?, no Did you went? Una acción breve que interrumpe otra suele ir en simple: I was reading when the lights went out. Was o were no se combina con la forma pasada para formar progressive: were walking, no were walked.
### Ejemplos
- **EN:** We visited the market on Saturday. --- **ES:** Visitamos el mercado el sábado.
- **EN:** It was raining when I left the office. --- **ES:** Estaba lloviendo cuando salí de la oficina.
- **EN:** While they were studying, their friends arrived. --- **ES:** Mientras estudiaban, llegaron sus amigos.
### Ejercicios
- **Prompt (EN):** Yesterday you finished your homework and then called your friend. Tell the story in order. --- **Model (EN):** I finished my homework and then called my friend. --- **Explanation (ES):** Dos acciones terminadas y consecutivas se cuentan en *past simple*. --- **Translation (ES):** Terminé mi tarea y después llamé a mi amigo.
- **Prompt (EN):** The phone rang while your mother was making dinner. Describe the interruption with when. --- **Model (EN):** My mother was making dinner when the phone rang. --- **Explanation (ES):** La preparación en curso lleva *past progressive*; el llamado puntual, *past simple*. --- **Translation (ES):** Mi mamá estaba preparando la cena cuando sonó el teléfono.
- **Prompt (EN):** At eight last night, your friends were playing chess. Describe what was happening at that time. --- **Model (EN):** My friends were playing chess at eight last night. --- **Explanation (ES):** Para una actividad en desarrollo en un momento pasado se usa *were* más *-ing*. --- **Translation (ES):** Mis amigos estaban jugando al ajedrez anoche a las ocho.
- **Prompt (EN):** Ask a classmate whether they saw the film last weekend. Use did. --- **Model (EN):** Did you see the film last weekend? --- **Explanation (ES):** Después de *did*, el verbo irregular vuelve a su forma base: *see*. --- **Translation (ES):** ¿Viste la película el fin de semana pasado?
- **Prompt (EN):** You were walking home and suddenly it began to snow. Connect the two events with while. --- **Model (EN):** While I was walking home, it began to snow. --- **Explanation (ES):** La caminata en curso lleva *past progressive* y el comienzo repentino, *past simple*. --- **Translation (ES):** Mientras caminaba a casa, empezó a nevar.

## Past Perfect Simple / Past Perfect Progressive
### Regla y forma
- **EN:** The **past perfect simple** combines **had + the past participle**; the **past perfect progressive**, **had been + the -ing form**. Had does not change with the subject and comes before it in questions.
- **ES:** El **past perfect simple** combina **had + participio pasado**; el **past perfect progressive**, **had been + verbo en -ing**. Had no cambia con el sujeto y aparece antes de este en preguntas.
### Formación
- **Name (EN):** Past Perfect Simple
  - **Affirmative:** subject + had + past participle
  - **Negative:** subject + had not/hadn't + past participle
  - **Question:** Had + subject + past participle?
- **Name (EN):** Past Perfect Progressive
  - **Affirmative:** subject + had + been + -ing form
  - **Negative:** subject + had not/hadn't + been + -ing form
  - **Question:** Had + subject + been + -ing form?
### Definición
- **EN:** Both **past perfect** forms look back from a past reference point to something earlier. The **past perfect simple** presents that earlier event as complete or counts its result: she had written three emails before lunch. The **past perfect progressive** focuses on how long an earlier activity had been happening or on its visible effect at that past point: she was tired because she had been writing all morning. Neither form means simply any action in the past; you need a later past point to look back from.
- **ES:** Las dos formas de **past perfect** miran hacia atrás desde un momento pasado de referencia. El **past perfect simple** presenta el hecho anterior como terminado o cuenta su resultado: she had written three emails before lunch. El **past perfect progressive** destaca cuánto llevaba ocurriendo la actividad anterior o su efecto visible en ese momento: she was tired because she had been writing all morning. Ninguna forma significa simplemente «cualquier acción pasada»: hace falta un punto posterior en el pasado desde el cual mirar atrás.
### Usos
- **EN:** Use the past perfect simple to make clear that an action was completed before another past situation or to highlight its result. Use the past perfect progressive to highlight the duration or continuity of an activity before a moment in the past. Since marks the starting point and for expresses the duration.
- **ES:** Usá el past perfect simple para aclarar que una acción se completó antes de otra situación pasada o para destacar su resultado. Usá el past perfect progressive para destacar la duración o continuidad de una actividad anterior a un momento pasado. Since señala el punto de inicio y for expresa la duración.
### Contrastes y errores
- **EN:** When the finished result matters, She had written the report focuses on the report being ready. When the time spent matters, She had been writing for hours stresses the process. Avoid using the past perfect for a whole narrative: the later action normally takes the past simple. A state like know usually takes the perfect simple, not the perfect progressive.
- **ES:** Cuando importa el resultado terminado, She had written the report pone el foco en el informe listo. Cuando importa el tiempo dedicado, She had been writing for hours enfatiza el proceso. Evitá usar past perfect para toda una narración: la acción posterior normalmente va en past simple. Un estado como know suele ir en perfect simple, no en perfect progressive.
### Ejemplos
- **EN:** By the time we arrived, the shop had closed. --- **ES:** Para cuando llegamos, el negocio ya había cerrado.
- **EN:** She was tired because she had been working all afternoon. --- **ES:** Estaba cansada porque había estado trabajando toda la tarde.
- **EN:** They had known each other for years before they became colleagues. --- **ES:** Se conocían desde hacía años antes de convertirse en colegas.
### Ejercicios
- **Prompt (EN):** The train left first; you arrived at the station later. Explain why you missed it with by the time. --- **Model (EN):** By the time I arrived at the station, the train had left. --- **Explanation (ES):** *Had left* ubica la partida completa antes de la llegada, expresada en *past simple*. --- **Translation (ES):** Para cuando llegué a la estación, el tren ya había salido.
- **Prompt (EN):** Your shoes were wet because you walked in the rain for an hour before coming home. Emphasize the duration. --- **Model (EN):** My shoes were wet because I had been walking in the rain for an hour. --- **Explanation (ES):** *Had been walking* destaca la actividad prolongada anterior al resultado pasado. --- **Translation (ES):** Tenía los zapatos mojados porque había estado caminando bajo la lluvia durante una hora.
- **Prompt (EN):** Before the guests arrived, your sister completed the cake. Express the completed result. --- **Model (EN):** My sister had baked the cake before the guests arrived. --- **Explanation (ES):** *Had baked* presenta la torta como resultado ya terminado antes de otra acción pasada. --- **Translation (ES):** Mi hermana había horneado la torta antes de que llegaran los invitados.
- **Prompt (EN):** At noon, the workers were exhausted after repairing the road since early morning. Explain their exhaustion. --- **Model (EN):** The workers were exhausted because they had been repairing the road since early morning. --- **Explanation (ES):** *Had been repairing* enfatiza la continuidad de la tarea hasta ese momento pasado; *since* indica cuándo empezó. --- **Translation (ES):** Los trabajadores estaban agotados porque habían estado reparando la ruta desde temprano a la mañana.
- **Prompt (EN):** At a reunion, you met a friend whom you had not seen for ten years. Describe the earlier gap. --- **Model (EN):** I hadn't seen my friend for ten years before the reunion. --- **Explanation (ES):** *Hadn't seen* expresa un estado de ausencia previo al reencuentro; *see* no lleva *progressive* en este sentido. --- **Translation (ES):** No había visto a mi amigo durante diez años antes de la reunión.

## Used to / would / was-were going to
### Regla y forma
- **EN:** **Used to + base verb** describes past habits and states; **would + base verb** can describe repeated actions. **Was/were going to + base verb** presents a plan that existed in the past.
- **ES:** **Used to + verbo base** describe hábitos y estados pasados; **would + verbo base** puede describir acciones repetidas. **Was/were going to + verbo base** presenta un plan que existía en el pasado.
### Formación
- **Name (EN):** Used to
  - **Affirmative:** subject + used to + base verb
  - **Negative:** subject + didn't use to + base verb
  - **Question:** Did + subject + use to + base verb?
- **Name (EN):** Would (past habit)
  - **Affirmative:** subject + would + base verb
  - **Negative:** subject + would not/wouldn't + base verb
  - **Question:** Would + subject + base verb?
- **Name (EN):** Was/were going to
  - **Affirmative:** subject + was/were going to + base verb
  - **Negative:** subject + was/were not + going to + base verb
  - **Question:** Was/were + subject + going to + base verb?
### Definición
- **EN:** These three forms refer to the past but answer different questions. **Used to** describes a former habit or state that no longer applies (I used to live there). **Would** recalls repeated past actions, usually after establishing a past setting (Every summer, we would swim there); it does not normally describe past states. **Was/were going to** reports an intention or prediction held at a past moment (I was going to call), without guaranteeing that the planned event actually happened.
- **ES:** Las tres formas se refieren al pasado, pero responden preguntas distintas. **Used to** describe un hábito o estado anterior que ya no se mantiene (I used to live there). **Would** recuerda acciones repetidas, por lo general después de situar el relato en el pasado (Every summer, we would swim there); normalmente no describe estados. **Was/were going to** informa una intención o predicción que existía en un momento pasado (I was going to call), sin asegurar que el plan se haya cumplido.
### Usos
- **EN:** Use used to to contrast the past with the present: I used to live there. To tell past routines in a story with an established period, would lets you vary the wording: Every summer, we would swim in the river. Use was going to or were going to when you talk about a plan seen from a moment in the past: I was going to call you, but I forgot.
- **ES:** Usá used to para contrastar el pasado con el presente: I used to live there. Para contar rutinas pasadas en un relato con un período establecido, would permite variar la expresión: Every summer, we would swim in the river. Usá was going to o were going to cuando hablás de un plan visto desde un momento pasado: I was going to call you, but I forgot.
### Contrastes y errores
- **EN:** Do not replace used to with would when describing a state like owning a pet or living in a city: I used to have a dog. In questions and negatives with did, the form is use to: Did you use to play here? Do not confuse was going to with an action carried out: I was going to leave can signal an interrupted intention, not that you actually left.
- **ES:** No reemplaces used to por would al describir un estado como tener una mascota o vivir en una ciudad: I used to have a dog. En preguntas y negativas con did, la forma es use to: Did you use to play here? No confundas was going to con una acción realizada; I was going to leave puede señalar una intención interrumpida, no que efectivamente salí.
### Ejemplos
- **EN:** I used to own a small motorbike, but I sold it. --- **ES:** Antes tenía una moto pequeña, pero la vendí.
- **EN:** When we were children, we would build forts in the garden. --- **ES:** Cuando éramos chicos, solíamos construir fuertes en el jardín.
- **EN:** They were going to travel on Friday, but the flight was cancelled. --- **ES:** Tenían pensado viajar el viernes, pero se canceló el vuelo.
### Ejercicios
- **Prompt (EN):** You lived near the sea as a child, but you live inland now. Describe the past state with used to. --- **Model (EN):** I used to live near the sea when I was a child. --- **Explanation (ES):** *Used to* expresa un estado anterior que cambió; *would* no reemplaza a *used to* para *live* en este contexto. --- **Translation (ES):** Antes vivía cerca del mar cuando era chico.
- **Prompt (EN):** Every winter, your grandfather told you stories by the fire. Describe the repeated past action with would. --- **Model (EN):** Every winter, my grandfather would tell us stories by the fire. --- **Explanation (ES):** *Would* expresa una acción habitual del pasado dentro de un período ya establecido. --- **Translation (ES):** Todos los inviernos, mi abuelo nos contaba historias junto al fuego.
- **Prompt (EN):** You planned to buy a bicycle yesterday, but you changed your mind. Describe the abandoned plan. --- **Model (EN):** I was going to buy a bicycle yesterday, but I changed my mind. --- **Explanation (ES):** *Was going to* señala una intención pasada que no se concretó. --- **Translation (ES):** Iba a comprar una bicicleta ayer, pero cambié de opinión.
- **Prompt (EN):** Ask your friend whether they played football after school when they were younger. Use did and use to. --- **Model (EN):** Did you use to play football after school? --- **Explanation (ES):** Después de *did* se escribe *use to* sin *-d*, seguido de la base del verbo. --- **Translation (ES):** ¿Solías jugar al fútbol después de la escuela?
- **Prompt (EN):** Your friends intended to meet you, but the storm stopped them. Describe their past plan with were going to. --- **Model (EN):** My friends were going to meet me, but the storm stopped them. --- **Explanation (ES):** *Were going to* expresa el plan previo; la segunda oración aclara que no ocurrió. --- **Translation (ES):** Mis amigos iban a encontrarse conmigo, pero la tormenta se lo impidió.
