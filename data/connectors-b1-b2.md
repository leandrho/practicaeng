# Connectors B1--B2

Conectores frecuentes para unir ideas, dar ejemplos y ordenar un texto o una conversación. Cada tarjeta agrupa el conector bajo la función para la que se usa.

## Adding information

- **moreover** --- in addition to what has been said --- y además, encima --- *The hotel was cheap; moreover, it was close to the centre.* --- *El hotel era barato; además, estaba cerca del centro.* --- *The course is short. Moreover, it is cheap, so I will sign up tomorrow morning.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **furthermore** --- in addition; used to add a stronger point --- además, y además --- *The room is small; furthermore, there is no Wi-Fi.* --- *La habitación es pequeña; además, no hay Wi-Fi.* --- *Furthermore, the staff were friendly and helped us with the heavy bags.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **in addition** --- besides; used to add information --- además, adicionalmente --- *The flat is cheap; in addition, it includes bills.* --- *El departamento es barato; además, incluye expensas.* --- *In addition, the price includes breakfast, so we can sleep longer.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **besides** --- apart from that; used to add an argument --- además, total --- *I don't want to go out; besides, it's raining.* --- *No quiero salir; además, está lloviendo.* --- *Besides, we have to wake up early tomorrow, so let's stay home tonight.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **what's more** --- used to add something surprising or important --- y lo que es más, y encima --- *She passed the exam; what's more, she got the highest mark.* --- *Aprobó el examen; y lo que es más, sacó la nota más alta.* --- *The restaurant was cheap, and what's more, the food was excellent.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **not only ... but also** --- used to add a second, stronger point --- no solo ... sino también --- *The hotel was not only cheap but also quiet.* --- *El hotel no solo era barato sino también tranquilo.* --- *Not only did we win the match, but we also played our best game of the season.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **additionally** --- used to add a further point --- además, a su vez --- *The hotel is cheap. Additionally, it offers free parking.* --- *El hotel es barato. Además, ofrece estacionamiento gratuito.* --- *Additionally, the buses run every ten minutes, so you don't really need a car.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **on top of that** --- used to add something even more annoying or impressive --- y a eso se le suma, y encima --- *The train was late; on top of that, we lost the reservation.* --- *El tren estaba atrasado; y encima, perdimos la reserva.* --- *On top of that, the hotel lost our booking, so we had to look for another one.* --- *Everyday conversation*

## Contrast and concession

- **however** --- used to introduce an opposite idea --- sin embargo, pero --- *The food was good; however, the service was slow.* --- *La comida estaba bien; sin embargo, el servicio fue lento.* --- *However, we can leave early if you are too tired to stay.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **nevertheless** --- in spite of what has just been said --- no obstante, aun así --- *It rained all day; nevertheless, we went for a walk.* --- *Llovió todo el día; aun así, salimos a caminar.* --- *The road was long and muddy. Nevertheless, we arrived before sunset.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **on the other hand** --- used to compare a different point --- por otro lado --- *This flat is cheap; on the other hand, it is far from work.* --- *Este departamento es barato; por otro lado, está lejos del trabajo.* --- *Living alone is quiet. On the other hand, it can be lonely sometimes.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **although** --- in spite of the fact that --- aunque --- *Although it was cold, we went to the beach.* --- *Aunque hacía frío, fuimos a la playa.* --- *Although the exam was difficult, most students passed it.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **even though** --- used to emphasise a contrast --- aunque, pese a que --- *Even though she was tired, she finished the report.* --- *Aunque estaba cansada, terminó el informe.* --- *Even though we booked early, the best seats were already gone.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **whereas** --- in contrast with the fact that --- mientras que, en cambio --- *Tom works at night, whereas Mia works in the morning.* --- *Tom trabaja de noche, mientras que Mia trabaja de mañana.* --- *Her brother loves football, whereas she prefers tennis, so they never watch TV together.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **despite** --- used with a noun to show contrast --- a pesar de, pese a --- *Despite the rain, the match continued.* --- *A pesar de la lluvia, el partido siguió.* --- *Despite the traffic, we arrived at the airport on time.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **in spite of** --- used with a noun to show contrast --- a pesar de, pese a --- *In spite of the noise, I fell asleep quickly.* --- *A pesar del ruido, me dormí rápido.* --- *In spite of the price, the hotel was full every night of the week.* --- *Everyday conversation*

## Cause and reason

- **because** --- for the reason that --- porque, ya que --- *We stayed home because it was raining.* --- *Nos quedamos en casa porque estaba lloviendo.* --- *Because the roads were closed, the bus left an hour late.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **since** --- because; used when the reason is already known --- ya que, puesto que --- *Since you are here, help me with this box.* --- *Ya que estás acá, ayúdame con esta caja.* --- *Since the shop closes at six, we should hurry if we want to buy the tickets.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **due to** --- caused by --- debido a, a causa de --- *The flight was cancelled due to the storm.* --- *El vuelo se canceló debido a la tormenta.* --- *Due to the strike, no trains left the station this morning.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **owing to** --- because of (more formal) --- debido a, por causa de --- *Owing to the heat, the match was moved to the evening.* --- *Debido al calor, el partido se trasladó a la tarde.* --- *Owing to the lack of rain, the farmers lost half of the crop this year.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **on account of** --- because of --- debido a, por motivo de --- *We left early on account of the traffic.* --- *Salimos temprano debido al tráfico.* --- *The concert was short on account of the storm last night.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **for this reason** --- used to introduce the cause of what follows --- por esta razón, por eso --- *The bus never came; for this reason, we walked home.* --- *El colectivo nunca llegó; por esta razón, caminamos hasta casa.* --- *For this reason, we keep a list of restaurants near the office.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **as** --- because; used to give an informal reason --- ya que, porque --- *As it was late, we took a taxi.* --- *Como era tarde, tomamos un taxi.* --- *As the shop was closed, we came back home with empty hands.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **given that** --- considering that --- dado que, teniendo en cuenta que --- *Given that it is Sunday, the bank is closed.* --- *Dado que es domingo, el banco está cerrado.* --- *Given that prices are rising, we should book the trip now.* --- *Everyday conversation*

## Result and consequence

- **therefore** --- for that reason --- por lo tanto, entonces --- *The road was closed; therefore, we took the motorway.* --- *La ruta estaba cerrada; por lo tanto, tomamos la autopista.* --- *Therefore, the meeting was moved to Friday afternoon.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **thus** --- as a result (formal) --- así, por lo tanto --- *He saved every month; thus, he bought a car in a year.* --- *Ahorro cada mes; así, compró un auto en un año.* --- *The data were checked twice, and thus the results are reliable.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **consequently** --- as a result --- en consecuencia, por lo tanto --- *She missed the train; consequently, she arrived late.* --- *Perdió el tren; en consecuencia, llegó tarde.* --- *The company cut costs; consequently, the service got worse.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **as a result** --- used to introduce the effect of something --- como resultado --- *It snowed a lot; as a result, the schools closed.* --- *Nevo mucho; como resultado, cerraron las escuelas.* --- *We trained every day. As a result, we won the final.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **hence** --- therefore (formal) --- por lo tanto, de ahí --- *The hotel is far; hence, we need a car.* --- *El hotel está lejos; por lo tanto, necesitamos un auto.* --- *Demand fell, and hence the factory closed early this year.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **accordingly** --- for that reason; in a suitable way --- en consecuencia, en conformidad --- *The plan changed; accordingly, we updated the calendar.* --- *El plan cambió; en consecuencia, actualizamos el calendario.* --- *Tickets are cheaper on Tuesdays; accordingly, we booked for that day.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **so** --- used to introduce a consequence --- así que, por lo tanto --- *The queue was long, so we left.* --- *La fila era larga, así que nos fuimos.* --- *It was raining, so we took the umbrella with us.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **as a consequence** --- used to introduce a result --- como consecuencia --- *He never cooked; as a consequence, he ate badly.* --- *Nunca cocinaba; como consecuencia, comía mal.* --- *The river fell, and as a consequence the boats could not move.* --- *Everyday conversation*

## Purpose

- **so that** --- con el fin de que --- para que --- *Speak slowly so that everyone can understand.* --- *Hablá despacio para que todos puedan entender.* --- *I wrote the address down so that you would not forget it.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **in order to** --- used before a verb to say why --- para, a fin de --- *We left early in order to catch the ferry.* --- *Salimos temprano para alcanzar el transbordador.* --- *She repeats the words in order to remember them better.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **in order that** --- used with a subject and a modal verb (formal) --- a fin de que --- *He spoke clearly in order that the class could follow.* --- *Habló claramente a fin de que la clase pudiera seguir.* --- *The teacher wrote the steps on the board in order that nobody got lost.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **so as to** --- used before a verb; in order to --- para, a fin de --- *Please whisper so as not to wake the baby.* --- *Susurrá para no despertar al bebé.* --- *We set off early so as to avoid the heavy traffic.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **with the aim of** --- with the intention of --- con el objetivo de --- *The school opened a club with the aim of teaching cooking.* --- *La escuela abrió un club con el objetivo de enseñar a cocinar.* --- *He moved abroad with the aim of improving his English.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **with a view to** --- thinking about a future goal --- con la idea de, con vistas a --- *They renovated the kitchen with a view to selling the flat.* --- *Renovaron la cocina con la idea de vender el departamento.* --- *We are saving with a view to buying a house next year.* --- *Everyday conversation*

## Time and sequence

- **firstly** --- used to introduce the first point --- en primer lugar, primero --- *Firstly, we need to check the list.* --- *En primer lugar, necesitamos revisar la lista.* --- *Firstly, wash the vegetables; then cut them into small pieces.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **subsequently** --- after something else has happened --- posteriormente, después --- *The flight was delayed; subsequently, we missed the connection.* --- *El vuelo estaba atrasado; posteriormente, perdimos la conexión.* --- *We visited the museum; subsequently, we had lunch nearby.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **meanwhile** --- during the same period --- mientras tanto --- *I cooked; meanwhile, my brother set the table.* --- *Yo cocinaba; mientras tanto, mi hermano puso la mesa.* --- *The train was delayed. Meanwhile, we drank coffee at the station.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **afterwards** --- after the event --- después, más tarde --- *We visited the museum; afterwards, we had lunch nearby.* --- *Visitamos el museo; después, almorzamos cerca.* --- *The class finished at six. Afterwards, we went out for pizza.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **in the meantime** --- during the period before something happens --- mientras tanto, en el interín --- *The repairs take a week; in the meantime, we can use the other car.* --- *Las reparaciones llevan una semana; mientras tanto, podemos usar el otro auto.* --- *The printer is broken. In the meantime, send the files by email.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **eventually** --- in the end --- finalmente, con el tiempo --- *They searched for months and eventually found the keys.* --- *Buscaron durante meses y finalmente encontraron las llaves.* --- *Eventually the bus arrived, after an hour of waiting in the rain.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **by the time** --- before a particular moment --- para cuando --- *By the time we arrived, the film had started.* --- *Para cuando llegamos, la película había empezado.* --- *By the time the bus came, it was already dark and cold.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **finally** --- after everything else --- finalmente, por último --- *Finally, add the sugar and mix well.* --- *Por último, agregá el azúcar y mezclá bien.* --- *Finally, the bus arrived after two hours of waiting.* --- *Everyday conversation*

## Examples and clarification

- **for example** --- used to give one example --- por ejemplo --- *Some fruits, for example apples and pears, are cheaper in autumn.* --- *Algunas frutas, por ejemplo las manzanas y las peras, son más baratas en otoño.* --- *Bring something warm, for example a jacket, because the nights get cold.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **for instance** --- used to give one example --- por ejemplo, como ejemplo --- *We need tools; for instance, a hammer and a screwdriver.* --- *Necesitamos herramientas; por ejemplo, un martillo y un destornillador.* --- *The shop gives no bags; for instance, you must bring your own.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **such as** --- used to list examples --- tales como, como --- *She speaks languages such as French and Italian.* --- *Habla idiomas tales como el francés y el italiano.* --- *Cities such as Lima and Bogotá are growing very fast.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **to illustrate** --- used to explain with an example --- para ilustrar, a modo de ejemplo --- *To illustrate the point, take the case of a small shop.* --- *Para ilustrar el punto, tomemos el caso de una tienda chica.* --- *The teacher drew a map to illustrate the idea for the whole class.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **in other words** --- used to explain something differently --- en otras palabras --- *The bus leaves at six; in other words, we must hurry.* --- *El colectivo sale a las seis; en otras palabras, tenemos que apurarnos.* --- *The room is not free; in other words, you have to pay for it.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **namely** --- used to give the exact names --- es decir, a saber --- *Two students, namely Ana and Luis, won the prize.* --- *Dos estudiantes, a saber Ana y Luis, ganaron el premio.* --- *Only one thing was missing, namely the final signature.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **in particular** --- especially --- en particular, especialmente --- *I like fruit; in particular, I love mangoes.* --- *Me gustan las frutas; en particular, me encantan los mangos.* --- *The soup was good, in particular the one with tomatoes.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **that is** --- used to explain exactly --- es decir, o sea --- *The shop closes at noon, that is, in thirty minutes.* --- *La tienda cierra al mediodía, es decir, en treinta minutos.* --- *Only adults can vote, that is, people over eighteen.* --- *Everyday conversation*

## Opinion and stance

- **in my opinion** --- used to give a personal view --- en mi opinión --- *In my opinion, this film is better than the book.* --- *En mi opinión, esta película es mejor que el libro.* --- *In my opinion, we should start the trip early on Friday.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **from my point of view** --- used to give a personal view --- desde mi punto de vista --- *From my point of view, the plan is too expensive.* --- *Desde mi punto de vista, el plan es demasiado caro.* --- *From my point of view, learning with cards is more active than reading lists.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **as far as I'm concerned** --- used to state your own position --- en lo que a mí respecta, por lo que a mí me toca --- *As far as I'm concerned, the meeting can wait.* --- *En lo que a mí respecta, la reunión puede esperar.* --- *As far as I'm concerned, we already have enough chairs for everyone.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **personally** --- used to give your own opinion --- personalmente --- *Personally, I would choose the smaller car.* --- *Personalmente, elegiría el auto más chico.* --- *Personally, I think the blue paint looks better on that wall.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **in fact** --- used to add or correct information --- de hecho, en realidad --- *He is not a doctor; in fact, he is a teacher.* --- *No es médico; de hecho, es profesor.* --- *The task was easy; in fact, I finished it in ten minutes.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **above all** --- more than anything else --- sobre todo, ante todo --- *Above all, we need to be honest with each other.* --- *Sobre todo, tenemos que ser honestos entre nosotros.* --- *The plan is slow; above all, it wastes time every week.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **to be honest** --- used to give a frank opinion --- para ser sincero --- *To be honest, I did not like the ending.* --- *Para ser sincero, no me gustó el final.* --- *To be honest, the hotel was much smaller than we expected.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **as I see it** --- used to give your own view --- tal como lo veo, según mi visión --- *As I see it, the team needs more practice.* --- *Tal como lo veo, el equipo necesita más práctica.* --- *As I see it, we have two options and neither is perfect.* --- *Everyday conversation*

## Summary and conclusion

- **in conclusion** --- used to finish --- en conclusión, para terminar --- *In conclusion, the course is worth the price.* --- *En conclusión, el curso vale la pena.* --- *In conclusion, I would recommend this hotel to families with children.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **to sum up** --- used to summarise --- para resumir --- *To sum up, we need more time and more money.* --- *Para resumir, necesitamos más tiempo y más dinero.* --- *To sum up, the trip was long but the view was great.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **in short** --- used to summarise briefly --- en resumen, en pocas palabras --- *The bus broke down; in short, we walked home.* --- *El colectivo se descompuso; en resumen, caminamos hasta casa.* --- *In short, the answer is no, but we can talk tomorrow.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **all in all** --- used to give a final judgment --- en general, en definitiva --- *All in all, it was a good year for the team.* --- *En general, fue un buen año para el equipo.* --- *All in all, we enjoyed the festival despite the rain.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **overall** --- considering everything --- en general, en conjunto --- *Overall, the results are better than last year.* --- *En general, los resultados son mejores que el año pasado.* --- *Overall, the service was fast and the food arrived hot.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **to conclude** --- used to finish (formal) --- para concluir, para finalizar --- *To conclude, I would like to thank the whole team.* --- *Para concluir, quiero agradecer a todo el equipo.* --- *To conclude, remember that practice matters more than theory.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **in summary** --- used to summarise --- en resumen, resumiendo --- *In summary, the plan saves money and time.* --- *En resumen, el plan ahorra dinero y tiempo.* --- *In summary, three points explain why the shop closed.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **in brief** --- used to state something shortly --- brevemente, en pocas palabras --- *In brief, we cannot afford the new price.* --- *En pocas palabras, no podemos pagar el nuevo precio.* --- *In brief, the report says that sales fell in every region.* --- *Everyday conversation*
