# Idioms y expresiones frecuentes B1--B2

Selección de expresiones relativamente comunes y útiles. Conviene aprender la situación en la que se usan, no solo la traducción.

- **a piece of cake** --- very easy --- muy fácil --- *The test was a piece of cake.* --- *El examen fue muy fácil.*
- **break the ice** --- make people feel more relaxed when they first meet --- romper el hielo --- *A joke can help break the ice.* --- *Un chiste puede ayudar a romper el hielo.*
- **call it a day** --- stop working for the day --- dar por terminado por hoy --- *I'm tired. Let's call it a day.* --- *Estoy cansado. Demos por terminado el día.*
- **cost an arm and a leg** --- be very expensive --- costar una fortuna --- *That car costs an arm and a leg.* --- *Ese auto cuesta una fortuna.*
- **get out of hand** --- become impossible to control --- salirse de control --- *The situation got out of hand.* --- *La situación se salió de control.*
- **give someone a hand** --- help someone --- ayudar a alguien --- *Can you give me a hand?* --- *¿Me podés dar una mano?*
- **hang in there** --- continue despite difficulties --- aguantar/no rendirse --- *Hang in there. You're almost finished.* --- *Aguantá. Ya casi terminás.*
- **hit the nail on the head** --- say exactly the right thing --- dar en el clavo --- *You hit the nail on the head.* --- *Diste en el clavo.*
- **in the same boat** --- in the same difficult situation --- estar en la misma situación --- *We're all in the same boat.* --- *Todos estamos en la misma situación.*
- **keep an eye on** --- watch carefully --- vigilar --- *Can you keep an eye on my bag?* --- *¿Podés vigilar mi bolso?*
- **learn the hard way** --- learn through an unpleasant experience --- aprender por las malas --- *I learned that lesson the hard way.* --- *Aprendí esa lección por las malas.*
- **miss the point** --- fail to understand the main idea --- no entender el punto --- *I think you're missing the point.* --- *Creo que no estás entendiendo el punto.*
- **on the right track** --- thinking or acting in a way likely to succeed --- ir por buen camino --- *Your pronunciation is on the right track.* --- *Tu pronunciación va por buen camino.*
- **out of the blue** --- unexpectedly --- inesperadamente --- *She called me out of the blue.* --- *Me llamó inesperadamente.*
- **pull someone's leg** --- joke with someone by trying to make them believe something false --- tomarle el pelo a alguien --- *Relax, I'm just pulling your leg.* --- *Relajate, solo te estoy tomando el pelo.*
- **see eye to eye** --- agree completely --- estar de acuerdo --- *We don't always see eye to eye.* --- *No siempre estamos de acuerdo.*
- **so far, so good** --- things are going well until now --- hasta ahora, todo bien --- *How's the project? So far, so good.* --- *¿Cómo va el proyecto? Hasta ahora, todo bien.*
- **speak of the devil** --- said when the person being discussed appears --- hablando del rey de Roma --- *Speak of the devil! We were just talking about you.* --- *Hablando del rey de Roma. Justo estábamos hablando de vos.*
- **take your time** --- do not hurry --- tomate tu tiempo --- *Take your time. There's no hurry.* --- *Tomate tu tiempo. No hay apuro.*
- **the last straw** --- the final problem that makes a situation unbearable --- la gota que colmó el vaso --- *That mistake was the last straw.* --- *Ese error fue la gota que colmó el vaso.*
- **under the weather** --- feeling slightly ill --- sentirse indispuesto --- *I'm feeling a little under the weather.* --- *Me siento un poco indispuesto.*
- **up in the air** --- not decided yet --- sin decidir --- *Our travel plans are still up in the air.* --- *Nuestros planes de viaje todavía no están decididos.*
- **better late than never** --- it is better to do something late than not at all --- mejor tarde que nunca --- *You finally started exercising---better late than never.* --- *Por fin empezaste a hacer ejercicio: mejor tarde que nunca.*
- **easier said than done** --- more difficult to do than to talk about --- más fácil decirlo que hacerlo --- *Changing habits is easier said than done.* --- *Cambiar hábitos es más fácil decirlo que hacerlo.*
- **once in a blue moon** --- very rarely --- muy de vez en cuando --- *I eat fast food once in a blue moon.* --- *Como comida rápida muy de vez en cuando.*
- **get the hang of it** --- learn how to do something --- agarrarle la mano --- *You'll get the hang of it with practice.* --- *Le vas a agarrar la mano con práctica.*
- **go the extra mile** --- make more effort than expected --- hacer un esfuerzo adicional --- *She always goes the extra mile for her clients.* --- *Siempre hace un esfuerzo adicional por sus clientes.*
- **make up your mind** --- make a decision --- decidirse --- *You need to make up your mind.* --- *Tenés que decidirte.*
- **no big deal** --- not important or serious --- no es para tanto --- *Don't worry. It's no big deal.* --- *No te preocupes. No es para tanto.*
- **on the same page** --- share the same understanding or opinion --- estar de acuerdo/entender lo mismo --- *Let's make sure we're on the same page.* --- *Asegurémonos de que entendemos lo mismo.*
- **ring a bell** --- seem familiar --- resultar familiar --- *That name rings a bell.* --- *Ese nombre me resulta familiar.*
- **take it easy** --- relax and do not worry --- tomárselo con calma --- *Take it easy this weekend.* --- *Tomátelo con calma este fin de semana.*
- **think outside the box** --- think creatively --- pensar de manera creativa --- *We need to think outside the box.* --- *Tenemos que pensar de manera creativa.*
- **time flies** --- time passes quickly --- el tiempo vuela --- *Time flies when you're having fun.* --- *El tiempo vuela cuando te divertís.*
- **worth a shot** --- worth trying --- vale la pena intentarlo --- *I'm not sure it'll work, but it's worth a shot.* --- *No estoy seguro de que funcione, pero vale la pena intentarlo.*
