# Idioms y expresiones frecuentes B1--B2

Selección de expresiones relativamente comunes y útiles. Conviene
aprender la situación en la que se usan, no solo la traducción.

-   **a piece of cake** --- muy fácil --- *The test was a piece of
    cake.*
-   **break the ice** --- romper el hielo --- *A joke can help break the
    ice.*
-   **call it a day** --- dar por terminado por hoy --- *I'm tired.
    Let's call it a day.*
-   **cost an arm and a leg** --- costar una fortuna --- *That car costs
    an arm and a leg.*
-   **get out of hand** --- salirse de control --- *The situation got
    out of hand.*
-   **give someone a hand** --- ayudar a alguien --- *Can you give me a
    hand?*
-   **hang in there** --- aguantar/no rendirse --- *Hang in there.
    You're almost finished.*
-   **hit the nail on the head** --- dar en el clavo --- *You hit the
    nail on the head.*
-   **in the same boat** --- estar en la misma situación --- *We're all
    in the same boat.*
-   **keep an eye on** --- vigilar --- *Can you keep an eye on my bag?*
-   **learn the hard way** --- aprender por las malas --- *I learned
    that lesson the hard way.*
-   **miss the point** --- no entender el punto --- *I think you're
    missing the point.*
-   **on the right track** --- ir por buen camino --- *Your
    pronunciation is on the right track.*
-   **out of the blue** --- inesperadamente --- *She called me out of
    the blue.*
-   **pull someone's leg** --- tomarle el pelo a alguien --- *Relax, I'm
    just pulling your leg.*
-   **see eye to eye** --- estar de acuerdo --- *We don't always see eye
    to eye.*
-   **so far, so good** --- hasta ahora, todo bien --- *How's the
    project? So far, so good.*
-   **speak of the devil** --- hablando del rey de Roma --- *Speak of
    the devil! We were just talking about you.*
-   **take your time** --- tomate tu tiempo --- *Take your time. There's
    no hurry.*
-   **the last straw** --- la gota que colmó el vaso --- *That mistake
    was the last straw.*
-   **under the weather** --- sentirse indispuesto --- *I'm feeling a
    little under the weather.*
-   **up in the air** --- sin decidir --- *Our travel plans are still up
    in the air.*
-   **better late than never** --- mejor tarde que nunca --- *You
    finally started exercising---better late than never.*
-   **easier said than done** --- más fácil decirlo que hacerlo ---
    *Changing habits is easier said than done.*
-   **once in a blue moon** --- muy de vez en cuando --- *I eat fast
    food once in a blue moon.*
-   **get the hang of it** --- agarrarle la mano --- *You'll get the
    hang of it with practice.*
-   **go the extra mile** --- hacer un esfuerzo adicional --- *She
    always goes the extra mile for her clients.*
-   **make up your mind** --- decidirse --- *You need to make up your
    mind.*
-   **no big deal** --- no es para tanto --- *Don't worry. It's no big
    deal.*
-   **on the same page** --- estar de acuerdo/entender lo mismo ---
    *Let's make sure we're on the same page.*
-   **ring a bell** --- resultar familiar --- *That name rings a bell.*
-   **take it easy** --- tomárselo con calma --- *Take it easy this
    weekend.*
-   **think outside the box** --- pensar de manera creativa --- *We need
    to think outside the box.*
-   **time flies** --- el tiempo vuela --- *Time flies when you're
    having fun.*
-   **worth a shot** --- vale la pena intentarlo --- *I'm not sure it'll
    work, but it's worth a shot.*
