# Idioms y expresiones frecuentes B1--B2

Selección de expresiones relativamente comunes y útiles. Conviene aprender la situación en la que se usan, no solo la traducción.

- **a piece of cake** --- very easy --- muy fácil --- *The test was a piece of cake.* --- *El examen fue muy fácil.* --- *The homework was a piece of cake. I finished it in ten minutes. Let's play football now.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **break the ice** --- make people feel more relaxed when they first meet --- romper el hielo --- *A joke can help break the ice.* --- *Un chiste puede ayudar a romper el hielo.* --- *Jo’s funny hat deserved a vote of thanks, for it was of general utility. It broke the ice in the beginning by producing a laugh, it created quite a refreshing breeze, flapping to and fro as she rowed, and would make an excellent umbrella for the whole party, if a shower came up, she said.* --- *Little Women — Louisa May Alcott*
- **call it a day** --- stop working for the day --- dar por terminado por hoy --- *I'm tired. Let's call it a day.* --- *Estoy cansado. Demos por terminado el día.* --- *We painted two rooms already. Let's call it a day. We continue tomorrow morning.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **cost an arm and a leg** --- be very expensive --- costar una fortuna --- *That car costs an arm and a leg.* --- *Ese auto cuesta una fortuna.* --- *My new glasses cost an arm and a leg. I need them for driving. At least they look good.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **get out of hand** --- become impossible to control --- salirse de control --- *The situation got out of hand.* --- *La situación se salió de control.* --- *The party got out of hand fast. Music was loud until three. Neighbors called twice.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **give someone a hand** --- help someone --- ayudar a alguien --- *Can you give me a hand?* --- *¿Me podés dar una mano?* --- *These bags are heavy. Please give me a hand. The stairs are too many.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **hang in there** --- continue despite difficulties --- aguantar/no rendirse --- *Hang in there. You're almost finished.* --- *Aguantá. Ya casi terminás.* --- *The exam is almost over. Hang in there for ten minutes. You studied hard all week.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **hit the nail on the head** --- say exactly the right thing --- dar en el clavo --- *You hit the nail on the head.* --- *Diste en el clavo.* --- *Your guess hit the nail on the head. The answer was Lima. How did you know?* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **in the same boat** --- in the same difficult situation --- estar en la misma situación --- *We're all in the same boat.* --- *Todos estamos en la misma situación.* --- *We are all in the same boat with this test. Nobody understands chapter five. Let's study together tonight.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **keep an eye on** --- watch carefully --- vigilar --- *Can you keep an eye on my bag?* --- *¿Podés vigilar mi bolso?* --- *It seems a shame to have neglected No. 14 all this time; and yet we may be sure that Mrs. Darling does not blame us. If we had returned sooner to look with sorrowful sympathy at her, she would probably have cried, “Don’t be silly; what do I matter? Do go back and keep an eye on the children.”* --- *Peter Pan — J. M. Barrie*
- **learn the hard way** --- learn through an unpleasant experience --- aprender por las malas --- *I learned that lesson the hard way.* --- *Aprendí esa lección por las malas.* --- *He touched the hot pan and learned the hard way. Now he always uses gloves. His finger still hurts.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **miss the point** --- fail to understand the main idea --- no entender el punto --- *I think you're missing the point.* --- *Creo que no estás entendiendo el punto.* --- *You missed the point of my story. It was about helping, not money. Listen to the end this time.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **on the right track** --- thinking or acting in a way likely to succeed --- ir por buen camino --- *Your pronunciation is on the right track.* --- *Tu pronunciación va por buen camino.* --- *Your English is on the right track. Your verbs are much better. Keep practicing every day.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **out of the blue** --- unexpectedly --- inesperadamente --- *She called me out of the blue.* --- *Me llamó inesperadamente.* --- *Marco called me out of the blue. We lost contact years ago. Now we meet every month.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **pull someone's leg** --- joke with someone by trying to make them believe something false --- tomarle el pelo a alguien --- *Relax, I'm just pulling your leg.* --- *Relajate, solo te estoy tomando el pelo.* --- *Don't worry, I am just pulling your leg. Your hair looks fine. We will arrive on time.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **see eye to eye** --- agree completely --- estar de acuerdo --- *We don't always see eye to eye.* --- *No siempre estamos de acuerdo.* --- *We rarely see eye to eye on films. He loves horror and I love comedy. So we take turns choosing.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **so far, so good** --- things are going well until now --- hasta ahora, todo bien --- *How's the project? So far, so good.* --- *¿Cómo va el proyecto? Hasta ahora, todo bien.* --- *The trip is great so far, so good. The hotel is clean and cheap. Tomorrow we visit the old town.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **speak of the devil** --- said when the person being discussed appears --- hablando del rey de Roma --- *Speak of the devil! We were just talking about you.* --- *Hablando del rey de Roma. Justo estábamos hablando de vos.* --- *Speak of the devil! We were talking about your trip. Come and tell us everything.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **take your time** --- do not hurry --- tomate tu tiempo --- *Take your time. There's no hurry.* --- *Tomate tu tiempo. No hay apuro.* --- *Take your time with the form. There are no wrong answers. I will wait outside.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **the last straw** --- the final problem that makes a situation unbearable --- la gota que colmó el vaso --- *That mistake was the last straw.* --- *Ese error fue la gota que colmó el vaso.* --- *The broken printer was the last straw. We lost a full day. The boss bought a new one.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **under the weather** --- feeling slightly ill --- sentirse indispuesto --- *I'm feeling a little under the weather.* --- *Me siento un poco indispuesto.* --- *You look pale today. Are you feeling under the weather? Sit down and drink some water.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **up in the air** --- not decided yet --- sin decidir --- *Our travel plans are still up in the air.* --- *Nuestros planes de viaje todavía no están decididos.* --- *Our holiday plans are still up in the air. Dad wants the beach, Mom wants the city. We vote on Sunday.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **better late than never** --- it is better to do something late than not at all --- mejor tarde que nunca --- *You finally started exercising---better late than never.* --- *Por fin empezaste a hacer ejercicio: mejor tarde que nunca.* --- *You finally called, better late than never. I waited all afternoon. Tell me your news now.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **easier said than done** --- more difficult to do than to talk about --- más fácil decirlo que hacerlo --- *Changing habits is easier said than done.* --- *Cambiar hábitos es más fácil decirlo que hacerlo.* --- *Waking at five is easier said than done. My bed feels too good. I need three alarms.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **once in a blue moon** --- very rarely --- muy de vez en cuando --- *I eat fast food once in a blue moon.* --- *Como comida rápida muy de vez en cuando.* --- *We eat pizza once in a blue moon. Mom says it is not healthy. But tonight is special.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **get the hang of it** --- learn how to do something --- agarrarle la mano --- *You'll get the hang of it with practice.* --- *Le vas a agarrar la mano con práctica.* --- *Skiing is hard at first. You will get the hang of it soon. I fell ten times yesterday.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **go the extra mile** --- make more effort than expected --- hacer un esfuerzo adicional --- *She always goes the extra mile for her clients.* --- *Siempre hace un esfuerzo adicional por sus clientes.* --- *Our teacher goes the extra mile for us. She stays late every Thursday. We all passed thanks to her.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **make up your mind** --- make a decision --- decidirse --- *You need to make up your mind.* --- *Tenés que decidirte.* --- *“You’ll stay in this room until you confess, Anne. You can make up your mind to that,” she said firmly. “But the picnic is tomorrow, Marilla,” cried Anne.* --- *Anne of Green Gables — Lucy Maud Montgomery*
- **no big deal** --- not important or serious --- no es para tanto --- *Don't worry. It's no big deal.* --- *No te preocupes. No es para tanto.* --- *I broke your pen, sorry. It's no big deal, I have another. Don't think about it.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **on the same page** --- share the same understanding or opinion --- estar de acuerdo/entender lo mismo --- *Let's make sure we're on the same page.* --- *Asegurémonos de que entendemos lo mismo.* --- *Before we start, let's get on the same page. Who brings food and who brings drinks? Write it here.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **ring a bell** --- seem familiar --- resultar familiar --- *That name rings a bell.* --- *Ese nombre me resulta familiar.* --- *That song rings a bell. Did we hear it last summer? It played everywhere on the beach.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **take it easy** --- relax and do not worry --- tomárselo con calma --- *Take it easy this weekend.* --- *Tomátelo con calma este fin de semana.* --- *Don't run today. Take it easy and rest. Your ankle needs one calm day.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **think outside the box** --- think creatively --- pensar de manera creativa --- *We need to think outside the box.* --- *Tenemos que pensar de manera creativa.* --- *We need a gift for Dad. Let's think outside the box. He hates normal presents.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **time flies** --- time passes quickly --- el tiempo vuela --- *Time flies when you're having fun.* --- *El tiempo vuela cuando te divertís.* --- *Time flies at Grandma's house. We play cards all afternoon. Suddenly it is dark outside.* --- *Everyday conversation*
- **worth a shot** --- worth trying --- vale la pena intentarlo --- *I'm not sure it'll work, but it's worth a shot.* --- *No estoy seguro de que funcione, pero vale la pena intentarlo.* --- *I never fixed a bike, but it's worth a shot. Bring your tools on Saturday. We learn together.* --- *Everyday conversation*
