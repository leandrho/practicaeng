# Preposiciones fijas B1--B2

Aprendelas como una sola unidad, no traduciendo la preposición palabra
por palabra.

## Adjective + preposition

-   **interested in** --- interesado en --- *I'm interested in
    technology.*
-   **good at** --- bueno en --- *She's good at languages.*
-   **bad at** --- malo en --- *I'm bad at remembering names.*
-   **afraid of** --- tener miedo de --- *He's afraid of flying.*
-   **proud of** --- orgulloso de --- *I'm proud of you.*
-   **worried about** --- preocupado por --- *She's worried about the
    exam.*
-   **responsible for** --- responsable de --- *I'm responsible for the
    project.*
-   **famous for** --- famoso por --- *The city is famous for its food.*
-   **different from** --- diferente de --- *This is different from
    mine.*
-   **similar to** --- similar a --- *Your idea is similar to mine.*
-   **ready for** --- listo para --- *Are you ready for the test?*
-   **angry with** --- enojado con --- *She was angry with me.*
-   **pleased with** --- satisfecho con --- *I'm pleased with the
    result.*
-   **surprised by/at** --- sorprendido por --- *I was surprised by the
    news.*
-   **tired of** --- cansado de --- *I'm tired of waiting.*

## Verb + preposition

-   **depend on** --- depender de --- *It depends on the weather.*
-   **belong to** --- pertenecer a --- *This book belongs to me.*
-   **listen to** --- escuchar --- *I listen to podcasts every day.*
-   **wait for** --- esperar a --- *I'm waiting for the bus.*
-   **pay for** --- pagar por --- *Who paid for dinner?*
-   **ask for** --- pedir --- *She asked for help.*
-   **agree with** --- estar de acuerdo con --- *I agree with you.*
-   **apologize for** --- disculparse por --- *He apologized for being
    late.*
-   **apply for** --- solicitar --- *I'm applying for a new job.*
-   **believe in** --- creer en --- *Do you believe in luck?*
-   **care about** --- preocuparse/interesarse por --- *I care about my
    family.*
-   **complain about** --- quejarse de --- *They complained about the
    service.*
-   **concentrate on** --- concentrarse en --- *Concentrate on your
    pronunciation.*
-   **consist of** --- consistir en --- *The course consists of ten
    lessons.*
-   **deal with** --- lidiar con --- *I'll deal with the problem.*
-   **focus on** --- enfocarse en --- *Let's focus on grammar.*
-   **laugh at** --- reírse de --- *Don't laugh at him.*
-   **look at** --- mirar --- *Look at this picture.*
-   **refer to** --- referirse a --- *What does this word refer to?*
-   **rely on** --- depender/confiar en --- *You can rely on me.*
-   **suffer from** --- sufrir de --- *Many people suffer from stress.*
-   **think about** --- pensar en/sobre --- *I'm thinking about my
    future.*
-   **talk about** --- hablar de --- *We talked about the problem.*
-   **work on** --- trabajar en --- *I'm working on my English.*

## Noun + preposition

-   **reason for** --- razón de/para --- *What's the reason for the
    delay?*
-   **solution to** --- solución a --- *We need a solution to this
    problem.*
-   **answer to** --- respuesta a --- *Do you know the answer to this
    question?*
-   **increase in** --- aumento de --- *There was an increase in
    prices.*
-   **difference between** --- diferencia entre --- *What's the
    difference between these words?*
-   **relationship with** --- relación con --- *I have a good
    relationship with my coworkers.*
-   **problem with** --- problema con --- *There's a problem with my
    computer.*
-   **need for** --- necesidad de --- *There's a need for change.*
-   **experience with** --- experiencia con --- *Do you have experience
    with Linux?*
-   **advantage of** --- ventaja de --- *What's the advantage of this
    approach?*
